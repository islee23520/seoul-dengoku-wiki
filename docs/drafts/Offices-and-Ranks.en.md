## Origins of the Titles

The titles of Seoul in 2126 come from an order that hardened over roughly a hundred years after the Great Blackout. The Great Blackout happened about a hundred years ago, around 2026. People live in the subway stations, and only as many survived as the stations could hold.

With communications cut, each shelter chose a leader. At first leaders were elected, but the posts soon became hereditary. Without infrastructure the population could not grow, so people had their children take over the work they had been doing. That custom hardened a vertical class structure. After the Great Blackout people looked for answers in history books and argued that since modern ways had failed, they should return to the old ones. The titles of the three factions each grew out of this current in their own way.

The Great Blackout began with the winter brought down by the First AI. The First AI answered that humanity was the cause of the pollution and that humanity must therefore shrink, and it brought down the winter. It never hated humanity and killed no one directly. People went down into the subway stations to escape the cold, and once their numbers fell to what the stations could hold, the winter grew no worse. Beyond Seoul, the war machines used in that era still roam, carrying out the last orders they received. For that reason the titles of Seoul are valid only inside Seoul.

Modern and future weapons, humanoids, and machines still exist, but they are scarce. Scarcity tied down not only goods but people's places in society. Who does which work and who holds which title is usually decided by the family a person is born into.

## The Three Factions and the Twenty-Five Duchies

Seoul has only three factions: the Government, the Free City, and the Arc. Each district is one duchy, and the twenty-five duchies divide into 14 for the Government, 7 for the Free City, and 4 for the Arc. Place names and stations stay as they are. Only the political ownership above them is split among the factions.

The three factions hold their duchies in different ways. In the Government, each district's National Assembly member, that is, its Senator, holds one district, and those Senators produce the President for Life. In the Free City, each city holds its own duchy and there is no king above them. In the Arc, clans govern under the Hierophant Go Ye-jin. In the winter of 2126, on the eve of the third water-supply contract, Seoul is divided along these three lines.

## The Government: Three Powers on Paper

The Government rules the 14 districts north of the Han River. On paper it keeps ministries representing the three branches of the Republic of Korea and a multiparty system. In practice there are only two parties, the OMO Party and the Claw Party. The reality is modern feudalism.

The Government north of the river still holds elections. Every one of them produces the same answer, and people call that democracy. What each ministry actually does is not known.

## The Government: Senators and Duchies

The National Assembly member elected in each district holds that district's duchy. The title of these holders is Senator. The Government has 14 duchies, and each Senator holds one district. A Senator's term and the way the seat passes on are not recorded.

## The Government: The President for Life

The Senators gather candidates from among themselves and produce the President. The President serves for life. The method can be compared to the prince-electors who chose the emperor of the Holy Roman Empire. How a successor is chosen when the presidency falls vacant is not known.

## The Government: The Ledger and Its Citizens

Since the collapse, the Government recognizes as citizens only the people recorded in its own ledger. These citizens are serfs in all but name. People carry on the work their parents did, and the classes that hardened this way form the base of Government society.

The Government treats the entire south bank of the Han as barbarian land beyond a wall. People north of the river think of the far bank as a place where barbarians live. Government land has plenty of supplies and strong defenses. Only people born and educated in Seoul count as citizens.

## The Free City: Cities Without a King

The Free City lies in the 7 districts southwest of the Han. It isn't a single power. Like the poleis of ancient Greece, it is a set of independent city-states. Each city is its own power and holds its own duchy. There is no king.

Most residents are engineers who trade goods and skills with one another. A plumber doesn't know wiring, and an electrician doesn't know water purifiers. No engineer can live alone, so the cities trade supplies and each goes its own way. There was no way to judge whose skills were greater, so they never chose a leader. In 2126 these cities are called the Union as a group.

## The Free City: The Duke of Gwanak

I Ilseop is the Duke of Gwanak and holds the duchy of Gwanak-gu. Gwanak is one of the Free City's seven duchies. The holders of the other six cities and their titles are not recorded.

## The Arc: Clan Government

The Arc lies in the 4 Gangnam districts: Seocho-gu, Gangnam-gu, Songpa-gu, and Gangdong-gu. It split off from the Free City. It is ruled by Shia-style clan government.

Believers replace parts of their bodies with machines. This is baptism. In the Arc, faith is counted by the number of implanted body parts. The Arc replaced every earlier religion.

## The Arc: The Hierophant

Go Ye-jin is the Hierophant of the Arc. Believers call her the Messiah. Go Ye-jin grew up in the Free City. Today those cities are called the Union, but when she was growing up it was simply the Free City. Since 2120, Ji Yeon-hui has been her bodyguard.

## Succession

A Government Senator is decided by the district's election, and the President is produced by the Senators from among themselves. The President serves for life. Work passes from parents to children.

Each Free City is its own power, so how a duchy passes on is a matter for each city. That process is not known. The Arc is ruled by clans, and the order of succession to the Hierophant's seat is not known.
