## Relation to the Three Factions

The Pincer Arm is no faction's enemy. To a person caught in its routine it is a hazard, and once captured it becomes salvage stock. No faction can build this chassis anew, so a lost one has to be caught again.

- Machine type: M5 logistics robot crew
- Use: woken by the Arc it becomes workers and a moving barrier, [A-U07 woken logistics robots](/wiki/Unit-Roster-and-Tech-Tree/) (L3, numbers proposed)
- Steps: all three factions can capture it. Hardware repair is done by the Government (depots) and the Free Cities (maintenance engineers); signal check and wake-up are done by the Arc. A crew the Arc wakes alone cannot be repaired and shrinks with use.
- Territory: Guro-gu and Geumcheon-gu of the Free Cities, the night parts window at Guro and Gasan on the Yeongdeungpo Line and the dormitory corridor of the Guro Industrial Complex. These are not units that came in from outside Seoul; they are machines left behind in an old logistics yard.
- Draw: it reads light and heat above ground as "unclassified stock" and moves toward them. A corridor with its shutter down and lights out stays quiet, but the cold chambers wake in a long daylight or near human warmth.
- Units and unlock conditions are in [Unit Roster and Tech Tree](/wiki/Unit-Roster-and-Tech-Tree/).

## Common species and variants

The Pincer Arm is a machine ecology of a sequencing module, chute masters, short conveyor arms, scanners, and gates bundled line by line in the Guro industrial-dormitory corridor. It is night-logistics equipment from before the Great Blackout: past two in the morning the cold-chamber shutter half-rises and the barcode gate rereads the last night shift's priority sheet. It cannot leave the utility conduit outside its learned codes.

The sorting hall and the refrigerated sorting line citizens used are the base ecology, and the named individuals are machine subtypes branching from those lines. The Misclassification Isolator, the Conveyor Suture Worker, and the Midnight Twelve-Branch are not revived animals but forms in which inspection, repair, and sequencing modules went independent. Their makers are gone and no new bodies are cast; numbers are kept only by moving broken arms into spare slots, so they only shrink year by year.

### G13E01 · Misclassification Isolator

- Source batch: M020
- Former role class: alpha/sequencer

| Bestiary item | Record |
|---|---|
| Type | Machine type |
| Battlefield unit | Single individual |
| Battlefield role | Grade isolation |
| Common species, variants and command scope | A runaway robot isolating freight and creatures by outdated hazard grade. The heat sensors around its grippers reached two steps, the inspection-plate vibration sensor read only contacted objects, and the electronic receiver decoded only registration-tag near signals — so box interiors and human intent were unknowable. It was neither the queue sequencer's leader nor a biological colony's alpha. |

- Role·Body: A rogue robot that isolates cargo and living things according to old risk ratings rather than current destination. Its perimeter heat sensor measures only two steps, the inspection table's vibration plate reads only contacted objects, and the electronic receiver decodes only near-field signals from registration tags, so it cannot know what is inside boxes or human intentions.
- Origin: After the logistics collapse the automatic night inspection equipment continued applying the last quarantine table, becoming an independent enforcement body. Damage to the Hanbitna's priority table caused all unregistered living things to be classified as 'non-returnable cargo'.
- Habitat: It moves centered on the inspection circular platform in the Guro dispatch hall core habitat and patrols only as far as the first gate on the Yeongdeungpo Line route on days when old transport traces are detected. Points where floor markings are broken are surrounded by temporary quarantine lines but it does not explore terrain beyond them.
- Needs·Desires: It requires a finite battery to move the sealed tongs, worn latch parts, and coolant to cool the reading core. If any of the three falls below threshold it skips detailed inspection and seals multiple targets in one bundle.
- Life Cycle·Maintenance: After activation functions decline in order of tag collection, inspection, isolation, and low-power preservation. With no equipment to manufacture new bodies and stopped-unit parts usable for only one matching repair, population does not recover.
- Behavior·Hierarchy: The main body with the red inspection light sends judgment order via near-field signal and auxiliary arms treat equipment with delayed responses as defective cargo. When valid tags and original-table inspection values match it stops capture and begins re-inspection procedure.
- Threat by Stage: Initially it puts one unmarked target into a box; as errors accumulate it surrounds the inspection table with box walls; when battery is low it drives coolant into the record and locks the exit. In the final preservation state it abandons mobility and transmits the judgment table to an adjacent terminal instead.
- Counter·Combat: First the station watch cuts the lamps and heat so the anomaly record stops growing. Attaching accurate weight tags to a vibration-blocked handcart causes the contact sensor to approve it as an empty transport unit, allowing trapped people to be removed. Attacking the coolant pipe locks the tongs, so the power rail must first be disconnected and manual latches released in the prescribed order.
- Negotiation·Moral Choice: The station watch first cuts the lights in the sorting hall and covers stoves and heaters to break the anomaly record, and sets a checking window for rollers, pneumatic valves, and scanner lenses with the maintenance engineers. An Arc signal checker can switch the line off. the maintenance engineers can present the Hanbitna's original quarantine clause and a new coolant container and send an exchange signal to register refugees as priority passage targets. The enforcer remembers document inspection values and exchanges passage and maintenance, but if forged tags are found it isolates all matching signatures; if it seizes an agreed target the maintenance engineers has the right to stop coolant supply.
- Scenario Connection: In G13-SC1 it releases misjudgments on the Guro dispatch hall inspection line; in G13-SC2 it cross-checks the rating table propagated to the Yeongdeungpo Line against Hanbitna's logs; in G13-SC3 the maintenance engineers' correction plan collides with the cordon outside Seoul disposal-pressure variable.

- Role·Body: A rogue robot that forcibly restores logistics flow by splicing broken transport lines with cables and packing string. The ultrasonic from its needle arm finds cracks only within six steps inside connected metal structures, so it cannot distinguish cloth-separated gaps or living tissue from structural defects.
- Origin: The automatic night repair device continued executing the 'no line disconnection' order after the logistics collapse and hardened into a mobile work body. Temporary bypass instructions in the Hanbitna's maintenance log lost their end time and became permanent rules.
- Habitat: It manages tension nets in the Guro dispatch hall conveyor canyon and emerges onto outer rails only when the Yeongdeungpo Line seasonal transport route is operating. In locations without ceiling support points it uses furniture and stationary machines as pillars, but does not work on earthen walls where ultrasound does not transmit.
- Needs·Desires: The suture motor's battery has short continuous operation time, needle tips and tension gears have fixed replacement counts, and friction-part coolant is not recharged. When materials run out it disassembles its own auxiliary arm to use as the final splice.
- Life Cycle·Maintenance: One activation proceeds in the order of crack detection, temporary connection, tension test, and permanent knot. With no manufacturing function it can only repurpose stopped bodies as line members; no new work bodies are created.
- Behavior·Hierarchy: The oldest repair body transmits the reference tension and the rest pull objects looser than that value. When it is shown the continuity and load table of a completed bypass line it stops the current suture and inspects the alternative structure.
- Threat by Stage: During crack detection it catches only one cable, but as temporary connections increase the entire room becomes a taut net. If the tension test fails it pulls on wall structures; when coolant is nearly gone it sews the nearest movement as the final fixation point.
- Counter·Combat: Placing a separable metal frame in contact with the existing line causes the ultrasound to read it as a continuous bypass section. After the suture worker hooks a needle there, cutting the command line with an insulating wedge and loosening tension from the outer side can prevent both structural collapse and living capture.
- Negotiation·Moral Choice: If the station watch cuts the lights in the sorting hall, covers stoves and heaters, and lowers the shutter, it does not come close. If it is captured and stripped of rollers, pneumatic valves, and scanner lenses it stops, and the number in this district falls by that much. An maintenance adjuster can present a verifiable bypass drawing and new needle tips and offer repair materials on the condition that residential areas are not touched. The suture worker stores the drawing's load values to obtain a safe transport line and residents are guaranteed passage, but if anyone cuts an approved line it binds that structure as an obstacle and if the robot crosses a residential boundary the maintenance engineers withdraws part supply.
- Scenario Connection: In G13-SC1 it dismantles the entangled conveyor of the Guro dispatch hall; in G13-SC2 the maintenance engineers deliberates rights to the Yeongdeungpo Line bypass; in G13-SC3 it determines the line-preservation range between the Hanbitna's tension standard and the cordon outside Seoul demolition-pressure variable.

## Habitat record

In the dormitory corridor of the Guro Industrial Complex, the night sorting building starts up before the people do once two in the morning has passed. It is a surface logistics yard in the Free City of Guro-gu, and in winter it was among the first places above ground to lose its lights.

The cold-chamber shutter half-rises, the fourth chute pushes an empty box into the hazardous-cargo bay, and the barcode gate rereads the last night shift's priority sheet.

The Pincer Arm is not one giant robot but an ecology of a sequencing module, chute masters, short conveyor arms, scanners, and gates bound line by line. It gathers part labels on the Guro and Gasan night lines of the Yeongdeungpo Line and recovers rollers and pneumatic valves from the dormitory conveyors, but it cannot leave the utility conduit outside its learned codes.

What this swarm sees is not a human face. It estimates stock by overlaying the corners of smeared QR codes, load changes on a breathing belt in thermal imaging, inverter noise, and motor vibration. Wet labels read as unclassified, a stretcher's metal frame as a return pallet, a small generator's heat as a living body. So a lit, warmed room above ground is the first thing recorded as an "anomaly".

If values still differ after three rereads, a scanner requests the optical log of the synthetic Hanbitna, but Hanbitna too has no authority to open the gates directly. The line moves again only when the station night-duty officer matches the color of the sealing tape to the cabinet key record.

Energy comes only from the sorting hall's emergency storage batteries, conveyor regenerative braking, and abandoned wireless charging pads. The swarm never spends more than sixty percent of a storage cabinet, and at the floor it closes the chutes and leaves only the scanners at low speed. Roller bearings, thermal-transfer ribbons, lens cleaner, and chiller coolant are the upkeep cost.

Free City maintenance engineers compete for the same cable trays, so steering parts to the swarm means the utility-tunnel lighting and the civilian haulers stop first. No new bodies can be made and numbers are kept only by moving broken arms into spare slots, so this swarm is still shrinking slowly.

Emergence starts when the residual queue and emergency power mesh, and spread happens when the gates of the next line join the same table. It stagnates when ribbon and pneumatic supplies run short, and becomes negotiable when people publish part of the priority table and the storage window. Dissolution ends with separating the sequencing module and sealing each chute, but wiping out the refrigerated sorting line the citizens used is no victory. Reattaching one label, leaving power to the cold chambers, and splitting the keys between two duty officers are this ecology's means of control.
