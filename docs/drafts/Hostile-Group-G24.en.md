## Common species and variants

On a midwinter night the wash-room floor is wet with water that has come out of the perfusion piping. The cry runs through the suction pipes and the drain trap and continues into the corridor. At the wet places are the big weeper, the pipe puller, and the Jangsan tiger.

While the big weeper’s cry spreads across the wash-room steel plate, the pipe puller is on its knees at the suction-pipe joint. The Jangsan tiger stands in the dark at the end of the corridor. The child’s cry from the Jangsan tiger’s mouth runs along the corridor and reaches the front of the wash-room door.

## Habitat record

The mouth of the person the night wash-room duty officer watched in front of the same recovery bath is open. From the teeth where the jaw used to strike shut, water has fallen as far as the chest. From the open mouth the cry has filled the wash room along the perfusion piping.

The cry behind the valve stays low in the suction pipe. After water in the recovery bath touches the night wash-room duty officer’s knees, the cry rings the wash-room steel plate. Beside the place where the big weeper stands at the rim of the bath, the pipe puller’s arm has gone into the suction pipe.

The big weeper is at the recovery bath beside the lukewarm perfusion joint. Steam touches the big weeper’s shoulder. Both hands are submerged in the water. One record board floats in the water beside the big weeper’s feet.

The pipe puller’s knees and hands are against the suction-pipe joint. The pipe’s water and a wet cassette are inside the pipe. Several pipe pullers on the same piping keep their chests toward the recovery bath at every joint.

The Jangsan tiger’s pale fur is wet with the water at the end of the corridor. The tiger pattern on the back is dark outside the night lighting and the long hair is stuck in front of the face. A child’s cry comes out together from the mouth and the pipe mouth. The long arms hang toward the flashlight on the corridor floor.

The wash-fluid drum is open beside the service rail. Empty cassettes and wet suction pipes are stacked on the conveyor. The handover order of the Central Intelligence Bureau wash duty and the White Cross corridor duty is written in the Duman River Far East Theater service-rail log and the Itaewon Interpretation Gate seal is at the end of the log. The Sewage Pump conveyor is stopped on that rail in the same order.

The wash-room door is braced with cassettes. The night lighting is on above the recovery bath. The night wash-room duty officer’s gloves are wet in two layers. One boot is inside the suction pipe and the remaining boot lies on the step of the automatic conveyor outside the wash room.
