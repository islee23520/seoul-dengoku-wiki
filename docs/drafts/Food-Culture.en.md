**A. Where rationing began**

In 2026, Seoul's food moved through Garak Market auctions, the dawn unloading at the Noryangjin Fisheries Market, convenience stores, and school meals. On the night of [The Great Blackout](/wiki/World-Unbinding/), refrigeration and remote ordering stopped. The leftover rice, the ice, and the water in the tanks went to concourse rations.

In 2126, residents collect rations at station windows. They receive water before rice. Facilities and markets reserve residents’ shares before trading. Distribution rests on the economy and production.

## The Station Ration Meal

**A. Ration windows and water supply**

In a dong with a subway station, the ration window was in the station concourse. People also helped distribute rations at school playgrounds and community-center windows; when the Yeongdeungpo treatment watch opened the tap, water filled the rice pots of the Sinjeong depot, and when the Amsa escort locked the canisters, the queues at Suseo and Garak lengthened that same afternoon.

**B. Porridge and ration stores**

The base of the window was porridge. The pots left by the school kitchens and shelter cookrooms of 2026 clung to one side of the concourse, the licensed markets split reserved portions from sale portions, and when the lower-floor ration stores of Jamsil and Garak failed to manage the heat of the grain sacks they became one room with the damp walls, running on into the coughs of [Ailments and Symptoms](/wiki/Ailments/).

In 2123, refugees from Bukhansan Bogukmun descended to Anguk.

**C. Blackouts and leftovers**

A transfer station cut from power stopped its freight too. Even with the tunnel sound, the traction current died, and Noryangjin's frozen stock was the first to melt on that outage; in summers when counterfeit medicine circulated, ice credit and water tests wavered in one line. When food waste was sealed off ahead of the citizen windows to protect the rations, the leftovers in the alleys called dogs and rats, and the way people and swarms contested the same remains was written by [People and Machines](/wiki/People-and-Machines/).

## Carp, Crucian Carp, and Catfish of the Tanks

**A. Running the tank-perch farms**

The tanks survived the Great Blackout. Remote safety control was dead, and the collector wells under the concourses, the depot drainage ponds, and the sedimentation basins of the treatment plants remained in human hands; the trade of holding on the station tank water the carp, crucian carp, and catfish that had been caught in the Han River and the reservoirs even in 2026 went by the name of tank aquaculture.

The wetlands outside the Yeongdeungpo and Yeouido sluices were that water surface. The sedimentation basin beside the Ttukseom pumps, the lower cells under the Amsa treatment plant, and the Changdong depot drainage ponds stood in the same line; the waterfowl occupied the shallow water, and the place where raptors looked down from the wires overlapped the edible cells. The watch wrote the section's water level to be divided among rice, birds, and trains, and the wetland-side colonies were the field of [G06](/wiki/Hostile-Group-G06/).

**B. Farm water and drinking water**

When excrement and feed from the rearing cells mixed with drinking water, cases of waterborne disease increased. Connecting water from a carp-rearing cell to the ration taps caused that evening's water-quality inspection to fail. Catfish ate organic matter on the bottom, so the water could appear less cloudy. Crucian carp multiplied quickly in shallow water, and the medical officer inspected the tanks and the medicine chest together.

The first floor of the Han's main channel stood close to abandoned space. Habitation pushed onto the third floor and above, cooking pushed inside the embankment, the sewage and floods along the tributaries turned playgrounds into detour embankments, and bringing fish caught beyond them to a window passed the inspection bench of the licensed market. Noryangjin's frozen remains and unloaded viscera became the dogs' night road, and the live fish of the station tanks read as protein that got through one more day without power.

## The Water Compacts and Water Rationing

A. The 2026 parts and water exchange

On November 6, 2026, two Yeouido vault representatives visited Yeongdeungpo waterworks. Han Jeonghun accepted two emergency generator parts and agreed to open the Yeouido water valve from 06:00 to 12:00 daily.

The dated water and parts record is in [the oral records](/wiki/Oral-Stories/#the-2026-water-and-parts-exchange).

Even with ration rice, without the treatment watch's signature clean cooking water was unavailable. If porridge was cooked with cloudy water, cases of waterborne disease increased. Counterfeit medicine also circulated that summer.

**B. Sluices and water districts**

Water without an Arisu mark is called guest water.

Garak Market has no water-treatment facility of its own. The Gwangam plant is in Hanam, a contested asset outside the opening area, and the Gangbuk plant is in Namyangju, limiting the water supply to Bukhansan Bogukmun, Changdong, Seoul Station, and Sangam. The public water-supply zones of 2026 do not coincide with post-collapse territory. Each of the three factions manages sluices guarded by people and authorizes ration windows.
