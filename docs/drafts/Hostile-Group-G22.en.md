## Common species and variants

The Flame Lantern carries a blue flame on the wet steel plate of the preservation-vault outer wall, on the siding cars, and at the tips of the spent-battery crystals. The flame is in front of the work lamp and floats low over the steam of the drainage channel. The person, with the flame attached, stands toward the rail and the wall or lies prone on the train roof.

Dokkaebi Fire is the several blue flames floating at the top of the outer wall. Bulgwisin is the person standing on the siding step below, with flame come out past the clothes. Hwama is the form lying prone on the grain-train roof, with the flame of the back laid over the row of cars. The three continue from the preservation-vault outer wall to the Seongha Group siding on the same tail of the meltwater pulse night.

**Life in the endless cold.** Flame Lanterns burn where wet steel meets spent battery crystal. Their flame is cold to the eye but real enough to melt frost, and in the long winter they gather at the vault walls where steam and old batteries meet.

**Threat and the cost of fighting.** Their flame can set stores alight near grain and fuel. The infected are a middle threat that grows in closed rooms. Infantry hold the doors in pairs, support teams bring medicine, isolation sheets and warm water, and missile infantry fire only when a line breaks. Recon drones are used to look into a room before anyone opens it, which saves lives but spends cells. War machines have no place here. The heaviest costs are medicine and the people who catch what they fight; ammunition spent on the infected is ammunition the station mourns later. Every watch knows the infected were people, and the duty log treats them that way.

## Habitat record

The outer wall of the underground preservation vault of the Government of the Republic of Korea at central Seoul Station and the Seongha Group siding of the Gangnam Line meet at the drainage corner. It is a night in the tail of the meltwater pulse. The Seongha Group siding yard duty is in the breaker hut. Water runs on the steel plate of the outer wall. The grain train of the West Sea Granary Theater stands on the siding, and condensate beads on the roofs. Spent-battery boxes from the Changdong Wheel Chamber lean toward the drainage channel, and blue flame spits at the tips of the white crystals.

Dokkaebi Fire floats at the top of the outer wall. The flames are blue and several, and one is low over the steam of the drainage channel. When the yard duty's work lamp lights the steel plate of a corner, the flame is already at the next corner. A person's shoulder and hand are inside the largest flame, and the fingertips are bright. The lower side of the flame is toward the wet steel plate and the steam of the car roofs. The place the palms face is the face of the spent-battery boxes where the white crystals stand.

Bulgwisin stands on the step of the first grain car. The flame of the head and shoulders has come out into the night air. The skin is split black, and light comes from the split places. The mouth is open, and the flame inside the mouth is below the chin. Steam stands on the rail under the feet. The sack cords are burned through. Bulgwisin's face is toward Dokkaebi Fire at the top of the outer wall.

Hwama lies prone on the roof of the grain train. The four feet span the joints of the cars. The head is toward the cars whose lids are pale. The flame of the back lies long over the roofs whose lids are curled black. The sleeper under the embers fallen from the nose is red. Spent-battery boxes are under the hind feet. Bulgwisin stands on the next step of the car that Hwama's head faces.

On the first night on the same siding a blue flame is a point on the back of a person's hand. On the next night the flame at the chest shows on the glass of the work lamp. On the night the grain train has come in again, the flame runs along the joints of three cars.

A hand rests on the fist marks of the outer-wall steel plate. A blue flame is fixed on the back of that hand. The same person's knees are toward the drainage channel at the foot of the wall. The flame at the shoulder is in front of the work lamp.

Dokkaebi Fire is at the top of the outer wall. Bulgwisin stands on the rail at the foot of the wall. Hwama lies prone on the train roof in front of Bulgwisin. The people with a point of flame on the back of the hand stand on the steps between the cars. Spent-battery boxes sit in the front row of the yard. Grain sacks have been moved off the rail from the car where the flame rests. Burned sack cords float on the drainage water.

A Republic of Korea Government duty tag and a Seongha Group duty tag hang side by side in the breaker hut. The Noryangjin Cold-Storage Guild outer-wall key is on the yard duty's patrol ring. The cell of the Elevator Shaft patrol lamp lies separate on the shelf. The grain train of the West Sea Granary Theater stands on the siding ahead of the Seongha Group yard inspection. The boots of the preservation-vault outer-wall patrol and the boots of the yard duty sit before the same breaker. The siding breaker is down. Fire sand lies the width of the rail in front of the spent-battery boxes. One glove is burned beside the rail. Grain is strewn between the sleepers. The work-lamp glass is split by heat. The yard duty is in the breaker hut with the inspection lamp lit at the siding entrance. The preservation-vault outer-wall patrol stands on the stair inside the corner.
