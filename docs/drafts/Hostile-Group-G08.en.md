## Relation to the Three Factions

The Dongdaemun Raiders are an armed band moving through the lower level of the night-wage corridor on Government ground (Dongdaemun). Stationless people and laid-off laborers are mixed in it. The filters and work lamps they carry off in sacks are emergency supplies of the White Cross. The White Cross is the Government's first-aid and transport body and has no doctor, so when it loses filters and lamps even the little first aid it does shrinks. The raiders do not only take supplies; they also seize first-aid workers and carriers and trade them back for filters as ransom. The Government can drive this band off with rifles, but it has no doctor to send a wounded soldier back, so it loses people every time it fights. A faction that takes captured raiders uses them as labor at the very bottom and does not raise their standing. The lines of cost are set out in [The Cost Structure of the Three Factions](/wiki/Cost-Structure/).

## Common species and variants

The Dongdaemun Raiders are a band of people in the lower windbreak alley of the Dongdaemun night-wage corridor. At the hour when the differential-pressure figures are written, the band stands divided between the positive-pressure doors and the ventilation-duct mouth, and the filters and work lamps are in sacks. The alley runs on into the White Cross corridor.

The band is divided into the raider chief, the raider elite squad and the raider scouts. The three are one armed band and share the doors, the sacks and the end of the alley. A captured White Cross member is kept by the elite squad on the inner side of the doors.

## Habitat record

The record board of the Government patrol duty officer lies on the shelf beside the latch of the Dongdaemun night-wage corridor. The remaining positive-pressure doors, the differential-pressure figures and an armed band are written together on it. The chief, at the front of the band, stands toward the door gap, and the scouts look from the end of the lower windbreak alley toward the duct mouth.

The Dongdaemun Raiders gather in the lower windbreak alley and along the row of remaining positive-pressure doors. In the season when the filter count falls, their footholds run on into the ventilation duct of the sealed side chamber on the Jegi-dong side. On a night when dust is piled at the entrance, the entrance curtains are layered and the band stands before those curtains.

The chief is before the differential-pressure board and the sack cords. The elite squad, on both sides of the chief, handles the curtains and the filter crates. The scouts are on the foothold at the end of the alley writing the curtain-layer count on a board.

Inside the sacks are White Cross filter crates and work lamps of the White Cross night-wage corridor. The filter count is written as the same figure on the White Cross duty officer's record board and on the chief's board. The work lamps lie across the mouths of the sacks.

A White Cross first-aid worker is not a doctor, and carrying one off gives the raiders no new medical skill. Even so, they reckon the person is worth some filter crates, and the chief names a number of crates as the price of the captive. The released person returns to work, but the filter count of that night is one layer lower.

## Linked scenarios

<a id="Hostile-Group-G08-heading-0033"></a>

### G08-SC1 · Filters on the Shelf

- Stage: 1
- Trigger: On an early-winter night the White Cross filter crates are before the positive-pressure doors of the Dongdaemun night-wage corridor and the raiders stand before the crates. The Government patrol duty officer is beside the differential-pressure board of the same door.
- Actors: government
- Ecological mechanism: The chief reads the differential-pressure figures and the elite squad takes the crate cords. While the filters are on the shelf the positive-pressure doors stay shut toward the outside.
- Choices: The patrol duty officer moves the filter crates to the inner shelf of the sealed side chamber on the Jegi-dong side. / The White Cross duty officer writes the crate count half on the shelf and half on the raiders' sacks. / Give up a carrier as the ransom and keep the crates.
- Outcomes: If the crates are moved to the shelf, the raiders stand before the doors and the positive pressure holds. If the count is split in halves, the crates are divided between the shelf and the sacks and the elite squad stands in the alley. If a carrier is given up, the crates stay on the shelf and the carrier is in the elite squad's hands.
- Moral cost: After the count is divided in halves, the White Cross protection filters remain as a single layer. After a carrier is given up, one patient transfer goes unstaffed and, until release, another carrier bears that share of the work.
- Source dossier: G08

### G08-SC2 · Curtains of the Night Wage

- Stage: 2
- Trigger: On a night when the White Cross night-wage duty officer is before the entrance curtains, the scouts stand at the end of the lower windbreak alley. The Government patrol duty officer is beside the differential-pressure board of the same entrance.
- Actors: government
- Ecological mechanism: The scouts count the curtain layers and write them on a board, and the chief sets the place of the night-wage sacks before the doors. The patrol duty officer leaves the differential-pressure figures on the record board.
- Choices: The night-wage duty officer keeps the curtains at three layers and opens the night-wage passage toward the inner side chamber. / The patrol duty officer writes the differential-pressure figures on both the chief's board and the duty record board. / Move the night-wage sacks onto the side-chamber shelf and leave the seized first-aid worker on the chief's board.
- Outcomes: If the passage is opened, the elite squad stands outside the curtains and the night-wage goods are on the side-chamber side. If the figures are written on both boards, the chief and the duty officer stand before the same board and the sacks remain at the doors. If the sacks are moved, the scouts are at the duct mouth and the first-aid worker remains as an item on the chief's board.
- Moral cost: Where the passage is opened, the night-wage goods remain in the sack count, and where the figures are written together the duty officer spends the night beside the door. While the first-aid worker is held, the White Cross is short one first-aid team.
- Source dossier: G08

### G08-SC3 · Dust at the Entrance

- Stage: 3
- Trigger: On a night when dust from the relay dock is piled at the entrance of the Dongdaemun night-wage corridor, a higher figure is written on the entrance differential-pressure board. The White Cross and the Government patrol duty officer are at the same entrance.
- Actors: government
- Ecological mechanism: The raiders layer the curtains over the dust and set the sacks at the entrance between the layers. While the curtains are layered and the filter crates are at the entrance, the positive-pressure doors stay shut toward the outside.
- Choices: The White Cross night-wage duty officer layers the curtains from the inner side of the raiders' hands and keeps the entrance on the duty side. / The patrol duty officer moves the relay crates that have no dust on them to the side-chamber shelf. / Write the entrance board toward the shut door and leave the raiders' sacks at the duct mouth.
- Outcomes: If the curtains are layered from the inside, the entrance is on the duty side and the elite squad stands at the outer layer. If the crates are moved, the dust-marked sacks are at the entrance and the protection crates are on the shelf. If the board is written toward the shut door, the band gathers at the duct mouth and only the patrol duty officer is left beside the entrance board.
- Moral cost: The sacks of the elite squad standing at the outer layer hold the night-wage work lamps, and after the band gathers toward the duct the passage of the sealed side chamber runs on into the raiders' foothold.
- Source dossier: G08
