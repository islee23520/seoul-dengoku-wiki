## Relation to the Three Factions

The Sangam Deserters are soldiers who walked out of the Government army with rifles, ammunition and supplies. The moment they leave the Government they lose their rank and whatever assets they held, and become ordinary citizens with no station to return to. The band has no one above squad leader and no officers, so it cannot lay combined fire with heavy weapons. Without a depot it only spends the ammunition and parts it carried out, so what it holds can only shrink, and with no doctor its wounded do not come back. So they steal food and fuel and hold depot watch staff for ransom to get by. A faction that takes them in keeps them at the very bottom and uses only their skills. What the Free Cities want most is an officer who can command tanks and heavy weapons; this band has none, so taking it in gains no more than a few riflemen. If an officer is ever found among deserters, that person becomes an object of contest between the factions. The units that the movement of officers and doctors opens are in [Unit Roster and Tech Tree](/wiki/Unit-Roster-and-Tech-Tree/).

## Common species and variants

The Sangam Deserters stand in the cold corridor of the Sangam cooling annex, wearing military clothing and carrying equipment and weapons. In midwinter they stand between the shelves of the corridor, and around the thaw they stand at the Achasan inspection window and the cold warehouse. In the room-temperature passage outside the inspection window the scouts assigned to the doorway stand, and the squad leader and the elite squad stand inside the warehouse and the corridor.

The deserter squad leader stands in the center of the cold corridor, the elite squad stands beside the goods to the left and right, and the scouts stand on the door side and the track side. The deserters in the three places wear the same military clothing and keep equipment with the same inventory marks.

## Habitat record

Beside the doorway of the Achasan inspection window is the passage that leads to the cold warehouse. At dawn around the thaw, the Government inspection watch officer stands beside the bar of the entrance. On the floor of the window, people in military clothing stand with an equipment pile set down.

In the equipment pile, fuel drums, cooling tools and cold-weather clothing with the Government mark are stacked together. Ammunition boxes lie in the front row of the pile, and rifles lean toward the window frame. The heat needle on the inspection-window panel sits in a high mark.

The squad leader stands between the equipment pile and the door of the cold warehouse. As in the midwinter corridor, the squad leader wears an ammunition belt at the waist and has set a radio set at the feet. A page of the inventory ledger is open in front of the boots, and the quantity in the removal column matches the number of drums in the pile.

The elite squad stands on both sides of the pile. One side holds cooling tools and leans on the shelf of the inspection window, and the other has set the fuel drums up to the threshold of the warehouse. They wear cold-weather outer layers over the uniforms, and inventory tags remain on the handles of the drums.

The scouts stand divided between the track-side door of the inspection window and the end of the removal passage. The scouts on the track side look at the outer glass and those on the passage side look at the floor that comes down from the cold corridor. The marks on the scouts' ammunition boxes match the removal column of the ledger.

Beside the Government inspection watch officer stands the Government inventory watch officer, who holds the ledger on which the removal column is written. The inspection watch officer stands inside the locked entrance, and on the day of capture is moved beside the shelf post of the elite squad. For release the Government depot must set a number of fuel drums at the door, and that number is written in the next column of the ledger.

A fuel train stands on the track outside the inspection window. The cold is loaded on the train, and a row of mineral boxes lies beside the equipment pile on the window floor. The scouts are lined up at the track-side door, and the squad leader stands beside the remaining fuel drums.

## Linked scenarios

<a id="Hostile-Group-G09-heading-0033"></a>

### G09-SC1 · Removal in the Cold Corridor

- Stage: 1
- Trigger: On a midwinter night in the cold corridor, fuel drums and ammunition boxes with the Government mark are at the feet of the deserters. The Government inventory watch officer stands beside the work lamp at the end of the corridor, and a contact from the Free Cities has come to the doorway.
- Actors: free-city, government
- Ecological mechanism: The squad leader stands before the inventory ledger page and the elite squad stands between the shelves and the squad leader's feet. The number of fuel drums is written in the inventory watch officer's removal column. The contact asks whether any officer is among them.
- Choices: The inventory watch officer bars the removal passage and writes the removal quantity in the ledger. / Hand a few riflemen to the contact in return for fuel drums. / Turn off the work lamp and stand inside the cold warehouse.
- Outcomes: If the passage is barred and the quantity written, the squad leader stands before the ledger page and the elite squad stands beside the shelves. If riflemen are handed over, those people become lowest-class hands on the contact's side and the fuel drums are at the squad leader's feet. If the lamp is turned off and the officer stands in the warehouse, the inventory watch officer is inside and the squad leader and elite squad stand beside the fuel drums.
- Moral cost: The fuel drums and ammunition boxes stay at the squad leader's feet, written in the removal column. The riflemen handed over gain no standing and become hands on another's ground. The answer that there is no officer is not written as a price for the contact.
- Source dossier: G09

### G09-SC2 · The Equipment Pile at the Inspection Window

- Stage: 2
- Trigger: At dawn around the thaw an equipment pile is on the floor of the inspection window and the heat needle sits in a high mark. The Government inspection watch officer stands in the doorway and the inventory watch officer holds the ledger.
- Actors: free-city, government
- Ecological mechanism: The elite squad stands on both sides of the pile and the squad leader stands between the pile and the door of the cold warehouse. The Government mark remains on the fuel drums and tools in the pile. The inspection and inventory watch officers stand in the same doorway.
- Choices: The inspection watch officer locks the door and stands the inspection staff inside the cold warehouse. / The inventory watch officer writes the quantity of the pile in the removal column and sets the ledger before the pile. / Give up the inspection watch officer to be held and take some fuel drums as ransom.
- Outcomes: If the door is locked, the inspection staff are inside the warehouse and the deserters stand beside the pile. If the ledger is set down, the ledger page lies before the pile and the squad leader stands in front of it. If the officer is given up, the officer stands by the elite squad's shelf post and the fuel drums are stacked at the door.
- Moral cost: The day-duty staff stand inside the locked door. The fuel drums and tools stay on the window floor, written in the removal column. Until the captured officer is released, that post at the inspection window stands empty.
- Source dossier: G09

### G09-SC3 · The Cold Train and the Track-Side Door

- Stage: 3
- Trigger: A Government fuel train stands on the track outside the inspection window with the cold loaded on it. The scouts are lined up at the track-side door. The inspection and inventory watch officers stand in the doorway of the cold warehouse.
- Actors: free-city, government
- Ecological mechanism: The train stands with the cold loaded, and few fuel drums remain on the inspection-window side and the warehouse side. The scouts stand before the track-side door, the squad leader beside the remaining fuel drums, and the elite squad beside the row of mineral boxes.
- Choices: The inspection watch officer locks the track-side door and stands the inspection staff inside the warehouse. / The inventory watch officer writes the count of the remaining fuel drums and mineral boxes on one ledger page. / Seize the train driver and hold the train on the track.
- Outcomes: If the door is locked, the scouts stand outside the locked door. If the count is written on one page, the ledger page lies on the doorway floor and the squad leader stands beside the remaining fuel drums. If the driver is seized, the train stands on the track and the driver is beside the elite squad.
- Moral cost: The track-side door is locked and the inspection staff stand inside the warehouse. The count of mineral boxes and remaining fuel drums is written in the ledger. Whoever seizes the driver draws Government pursuit each time a train stops.
- Source dossier: G09
