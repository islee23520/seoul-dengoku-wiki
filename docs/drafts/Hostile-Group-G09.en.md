## Common species and variants

The Sangam Deserters stand in the cold corridor of the Sangam cooling annex, wearing military clothing and carrying equipment and weapons. In midwinter they stand between the shelves of the corridor, and around the thaw they stand at the Achasan inspection window and the cold warehouse. In the room-temperature passage outside the inspection window the deserter scouts assigned to the doorway stand, and the deserter squad leader and the deserter elite squad stand inside the warehouse and the corridor.

The deserter squad leader stands in the center of the cold corridor, the deserter elite squad stands beside the goods to the squad leader's left and right, and the deserter scouts stand on the door side and the track side. When the deserter elite squad sets the goods at the Achasan inspection window, the deserter squad leader stands beside the remaining fuel drums and the deserter scouts stand at the end of that passage. The deserters in the three places wear the same military clothing and keep equipment with the same inventory marks.

**Life in the endless cold.** The Sangam Deserters keep to the cold corridor because cold preserves the goods they trade. They carry military clothing built for winter and weapons they keep oiled against frost, and they come out to the inspection windows only when heat on the panels says the guards are warm and slow.

**Threat and the cost of fighting.** They are trained soldiers with working weapons. People are the most expensive threat a station can face, because they shoot back with the same scarce ammunition. Infantry hold the doors and corridors, missile infantry and marksmen cover the long stretches, and recon drone teams find the group before anyone walks into its sightline. Strike drones are kept back: a drone lost to a net or a jammer is parts and cells the station won't see again. War machines are rarely worth the fuel in a tunnel. Every fight costs rounds, medicine and lives on both sides, and fighters with implants risk parts no workshop can easily spare. That's why the watch talks first, and fighting is the last answer rather than the first.

## Habitat record

Beside the doorway of the Achasan inspection window is the passage that leads to the cold warehouse. At dawn around the thaw, the inspection watch of the Achasan Gate stands beside the bar of the entrance. On the floor of the window, people in military clothing stand with an equipment pile set down.

In the equipment pile, fuel drums, cooling tools, and cold-weather clothing marked by the Seoul Station Inventory House are stacked together. Ammunition boxes lie in the front row of the pile, and rifles lean toward the window frame. The heat needle on the inspection-window panel sits in a high mark.

The deserter squad leader stands between the equipment pile and the door of the cold warehouse. As in the midwinter corridor, the squad leader wears an ammunition belt at the waist and has set a radio set at the feet. A page of the inventory ledger is open in front of the squad leader's boots, and the quantity in the removal column matches the number of drums in the pile.

The deserter elite squad stands on both sides of the pile. The personnel on one side hold cooling tools and lean on the shelf of the inspection window, and the personnel on the other side have set the fuel drums up to the threshold of the warehouse. The elite squad wears cold-weather outer layers over the uniforms, and inventory tags remain on the handles of the drums.

The deserter scouts stand divided between the track-side door of the inspection window and the end of the removal passage. The scouts on the track side look at the outer glass, and the scouts on the passage side look at the floor that comes down from the cold corridor. The marks on the deserter scouts' ammunition boxes match the removal column of the ledger.

Beside the inspection watch of the Achasan Gate stand the inventory watch of the Seoul Station Inventory House and the equity watch of the Yeouido Investment Federation. The inventory watch holds the ledger on which the removal column is written, and the equity watch holds the page on which the cold-quantity column is written. Personnel on the Joharam side stand at the wall between the work lamp and the warehouse door.

A mineral and fuel train of the Duman River Far East Theater stands on the track outside the inspection window. The cold is loaded on the train, and a row of mineral boxes lies beside the equipment pile on the window floor. The deserter scouts are lined up at the track-side door, and the deserter squad leader stands beside the remaining fuel drums.

The inspection watch of the Achasan Gate stands inside the locked entrance. The ledger of the inventory watch of the Seoul Station Inventory House is open on the floor of the doorway, and the personnel on the Joharam side stand beside the ledger. The equity watch of the Yeouido Investment Federation stands at the inner wall of the cold warehouse, holding the page on which the cold column is written toward the train.
