## Common species and variants

The Fire-Leg Train moved between the southwestern exit lines and the Yongsan retired and turnback lines, repeating unfinished maintenance orders with a defect-map controller and function-divided consists. It was no combat train and could not find new routes beyond the sealed main lines by itself. It could grind temporary crossing plates down as cracks, or pass a real crack by classifying it as a joint.

An unsealed defect map and exit-line power were the base ecology, and from the defect-map locomotive to the twin-torch head car the units were divided like gauge wheels, sleeper flats, insulated joints, and point wedges. No form revived an animal; it only coupled abandoned maintenance cars into existing consists and produced no new car bodies. Later cars absent from this deployment also remained subtypes of the same consists and were not invented here.

## Habitat record

At the end of the southwestern outbound track, the grinding stone of a maintenance car bit into an already smooth joint again and threw sparks.

Pushed by XT03 transshipment vibration, the defect map in the onboard port mixed real cracks with old coordinates, and the welding car and sleeper car that followed received the same kilometer post as their work target.

The Fire-Leg Train was not a combat train but an ecology of defect-map controllers and function-specific consists repeating unfinished maintenance orders.

It went back and forth between the outbound track of the Yeongdeungpo Line depot and the Yongsan retired and turnaround lines of the Wangsimni Union, but could not find new routes outside the sealed main line on its own.

Track-gauging wheels, ultrasonic flaw detection, axle heat, traction-power arc sounds, and point contacts were its senses.

It could see a temporary crossing platform as a crack and grind it away, or classify a true crack as a joint and pass it by.

When transshipment vibration and welding arcs overlapped, it dropped into waiting for human verification before cutting.

F02 Switchgear Panel recorded the two waveforms separately but could not correct the defect coordinates.

Only the HP04 operations supervisor, who kept the stop-service orders stamped with the source hash, approved departures and stops by kilometer post.

Depot traction power, onboard batteries, welding-generator fuel cells, and regenerative braking were its energy sources.

Welding wire, grinding stones, sleeper anchors, insulated joints, lubricant, and coolant were consumed.

A car that hit the fuel lower limit or axle overheating was dropped from the consist and left only its gauging running on a designated siding.

It could not produce new car bodies and only coupled abandoned maintenance cars to existing consists.

Cutting the traction power entirely stopped the wrongful cuts, but also removed the gauging that found cracks at night and the emergency welding.

The next maintenance window opened only when the supervisor recovered the previous stop-service order and inserted a new exclusion list.

Its emergence occurred when an unsealed defect map meshed with the outbound traction power, and its spread when a neighboring consist received the same coordinate list.

Wire exhaustion and overheating made it stall, and when the maintenance windows and sealed sections were disclosed, negotiation became possible.

Breaking it up was not burning down the depot but a process of recovering the wrong map, locking the points, and separating the consists onto the sidings.

The gauging wheels and manual towing had to be kept to preserve the ability to look after real cracks.

### G18-SC3 · The Return Without Sparks

Before the welding car and sleeper car crossed the points of the sealed section, only the main-line breaker was opened and the siding gauging power was kept on.

The HP04 librarian pulled the wrongly connected map from the onboard port, and F02 left only the waveforms the manual tow car would read.

Blowing up the consist ended the wrongful work at once, but the equipment to repair retired-line collapses and main-line cracks through the night vanished as well.

Coexistence was the choice to accept slow supervision checking the source hash at each kilometer post and the cost of emergency trains taking detours.
