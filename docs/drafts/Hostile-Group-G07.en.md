## Relation to the Three Factions

The Yongsan Raiders are a band of people who hold no station, gathered on Government ground (Yongsan). A person without a station is an ordinary citizen, and these are the ones who sit out the winter on the high floors outside the shutters. They are after food sacks and sealed boxes, and when food runs short they take a ration worker away and trade the person back for food as ransom. What they carry is little more than sticks and box hooks. In a straight fight they cannot beat a Government rifle squad or patrol shield squad. But the Government can give only first aid, so a soldier badly hurt in a clash with them does not come back. That is why Government watch staff try to stop them first with sealed dispatch slots and inventory records rather than with force. A faction that takes captured raiders uses them as useful labor at the very bottom and does not raise their standing. The lines of cost are set out in [The Cost Structure of the Three Factions](/wiki/Cost-Structure/).

## Common species and variants

The Yongsan Raiders are people, and they stand at the leak pallets of the lower level of the Yongsan stabling siding and of the Itaewon passage alley. In the rainy season and the thaw they move along the ladders and car doors of the Yongsan–Itaewon transfer freight elevator shaft, and in the dry season they gather toward the wall of the same lower level. In their hands are food sacks, sealed boxes and water cans, and when they hold a captured ration worker the elite squad keeps that person standing against the shaft wall.

The raider chief, the raider elite squad and the raider scouts are three places in one alley. The chief at the inner pallets sets the box count and the ransom in food, the elite squad handles the doors, the sacks and the captive, and the scouts watch for the arrival of cars from the entrance.

## Habitat record

The lower level of the Yongsan stabling siding on a rainy-season night is the place where the station duty staff of the Government first recorded the Yongsan Raiders. People stand in the rows of leak pallets with rods at their waists and box hooks in their hands. The lid of the food box in front of them is open, and the same number on the inventory board is written in the slot of that night.

From the duty staff's side, the scouts watch the entrance of the Itaewon passage alley and the chief watches the box count at the inner pallets. The elite squad's hooks are caught on the corner of the transfer freight elevator door. The duty staff write the state of the door and the box count on the same line of the inventory board.

In the rainy season and the thaw the people are on the ladder and the threshold along the shaft. In the dry season their footholds are within three rows of leak pallets, and the inventory board records the rows where people stood in each season. A salt stain runs from the floor of the refrigerated car to the leak pallets.

After the duty staff lock the shaft door, the elevator car sits in the lower level of the Yongsan stabling siding and the lifted sealed boxes stand against the wall. On a night when a ration worker is held, the number of ransom sacks is written in the next slot of the inventory board, and after release a line is drawn through that slot.

## Linked scenarios

<a id="Hostile-Group-G07-heading-0033"></a>

### G07-SC1 · Sealed Boxes at the Leak Pallets

- Stage: 1
- Trigger: On a rainy-season night the transfer freight elevator is stopped in the Itaewon passage alley and a sealed Government box stands before the leak pallets. The scouts stand in the row of pallets.
- Actors: government
- Ecological mechanism: The duty staff count the boxes at the shaft door. The chief is at the inner pallets and the elite squad holds the box hooks.
- Choices: Leave the elevator car in the lower level, lock the shaft door and set the boxes against the wall. / Pass the boxes out of the alley in the order of the inventory board. / Empty the duty post and leave the boxes where the raiders stand.
- Outcomes: If the first is chosen the boxes are in the lower level and the raiders stand before the locked shaft door. If the second, the boxes are out of the alley with their sealing bands still on. If the third, the boxes are on the pallets and the mouths of the elite squad's sacks are open.
- Moral cost: After the door is locked, that day's water-can count on the board is lower by the number of boxes. After the boxes are passed on in order, the raiders remain in the alley empty-handed. After the post is left empty, the same number is written in the raiders' sack column.
- Source dossier: G07

### G07-SC2 · The Freight Car on a Thaw Morning

- Stage: 2
- Trigger: On a thaw morning a Government freight car enters the transfer freight elevator shaft with a sealing band on its door. The elite squad stands on the rungs of the ladder.
- Actors: government
- Ecological mechanism: Damp on the floor of the car runs on to the leak pallets. The box counts of the inventory board and the ration slip differ, and the chief sets sacks beside the car to the amount of the difference. The ration worker on the car stands by the door.
- Choices: Match the ration slip to the inventory board and lower the difference in boxes to the lower level. / Press the sealing band again and send the car to the upper stopping line of the shaft. / Give up the ration worker and load the difference in boxes into the raiders' sacks.
- Outcomes: If the first is chosen the difference in boxes is against the lower wall and the two slips agree. If the second, the car is at the upper stopping line and the raiders remain on the rungs. If the third, the ration worker is in the elite squad's hands and the sack mouths are open.
- Moral cost: After the boxes are lowered, the food sacks of the alley are left on the board as the remainder by the amount of the difference. After the car is sent up, the empty threshold runs on to the number of the next car. After the worker is given up, the ransom is written as a number of sacks and is not erased from the board until the person is released.
- Source dossier: G07

### G07-SC3 · Food Sacks in the Refrigerated Car

- Stage: 3
- Trigger: On the night the rainy season turns to the thaw, a Government refrigerated car has been pushed as far as the Itaewon passage alley. The raiders gather before the car door.
- Actors: government
- Ecological mechanism: A salt stain runs from the floor of the refrigerated car to the leak pallets. Sealed boxes and food sacks lie in the same row. The chief counts the sacks, the elite squad props the door, and the scouts stand at the alley entrance.
- Choices: Leave the car in the lower level and let the duty staff keep only the sack count on the inventory board. / Pass the sealed boxes out of the alley first and release the sacks in order. / Leave the car door open and set the food sacks on the side where the raiders stand.
- Outcomes: If the first is chosen the car is in the lower level and the sack count is written in the next column of the board. If the second, the sealed boxes are out of the alley and the sack count agrees on one board. If the third, the sacks are before the car door and the mouths of the elite squad's sacks are open.
- Moral cost: After only the count is kept, the food sacks are in the lower level and that day's water-can column is written next to that number. After the sealed boxes are passed first, the scouts at the entrance stand in a single line. After the door is left open, the same sack numbers are written in the raiders' column.
- Source dossier: G07
