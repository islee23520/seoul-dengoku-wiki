**A. Purpose of the Values Scale**

Temperament, ambitions, fears and values influence a person's judgment and actions. Values are considered using ten criteria: authority, openness, force, material concerns, community, principle, disclosure, qualification, decentralization and reform. Values can lead people to choose different methods when faced with the same problem. The same criteria describe an organization's values, which are reflected in its decisions and operating practices. Its policies may change when leadership changes.

The scale runs from −100 to 100. Zero means that the axis has not yet tipped in either direction; it does not treat indifference as a virtue. Desires and preferences are recorded separately from values. The values are recorded in [values-cast.json](https://github.com/islee23520/seoul-dengoku-wiki/blob/main/lore/name-pools/values-cast.json) and [values-orgs.json](https://github.com/islee23520/seoul-dengoku-wiki/blob/main/lore/name-pools/values-orgs.json).

**B. Persons and Organizations**

## The Ten Person Value Cells

**A. Scale Direction**

Left is negative, right is positive.

| # | Axis | −100 | +100 | Decisions affected |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Authority | Equality · consensus | Command · magistracy | Emergency powers, water cutoffs, protection governments |
| 2 | Openness | Exclusion · quarantine | Hospitality · citizenship | Migrants, external theaters, corridors |
| 3 | Force | Peace · convoy | Preemption · occupation | Deployments, blockades, sieges |
| 4 | Material | Rite · sanctity of records | Stock · profit | Cooling keys, auctions, parts |
| 5 | Community | Personal survival | Family · guild | Registers, leagues, hostages |
| 6 | Principle | Expediency · compromise | Charter wording | Treaty breaches, false broadcasts |
| 7 | Disclosure | Secret ledgers | Cross-verified openness | Wills, price bulletins, transmissions |
| 8 | Qualification | Heredity · bloodline | Examination · apprenticeship | Succession, elders, stewards |
| 9 | Decentralization | Central monopoly | Living-sphere partition | Water federations, joint negotiation |
| 10 | Reform | Preservation · oral record | Charter amendment | Empty-century debts, trusts |

## Person Desires and Tastes

**A. Nature of Desires**

Values describe a person's stance on public issues. Desires describe **with whom and how a person wants to form relationships**, and influence decisions alongside ambitions and fears.

| # | Axis | −100 | +100 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Longing | Low · restraint | High |
| 2 | Possessiveness | Sharing · open seats | Monopoly · jealousy |
| 3 | Risk | Safety · predictability | Danger · the forbidden |
| 4 | Display | Private | Public |
| 5 | Duration | The moment | Long companionship |

**B. Detailed Disposition Categories**

Orientation: opposite sex / same sex / both sexes / none / fluid. Bonding: monogamy / multi-partner / contract cohabitation / non-exclusive / none.

The five desire scales are longing, possessiveness, risk, display and duration. Orientation and bonding are recorded separately from these scales.

## Organization Values

**A. Criteria for Organizational Values**

Organization values were likewise built on the **same ten axes** as persons. The numbers were scored against the current charter, council majorities, and ledger practice. Within the Government, the authority number an individual Senator carried could differ from the authority number of the Government itself, and it was precisely in that difference that issues budded.

## The Nine Organization Policy Cells

**A. Policy Choices**

Organization policies have nine categories: war, migrants, water, labor, records, technology, trade, succession and bonding. Each category has its own choices.

| Slot | Choices | What it looked like in Seoul |
|---|---|---|
| War | Defense / limited war / preemption | Garrison only / protection army / preemptive occupation |
| Migrants | Expulsion / quarantine admission / citizenship | Gate registers |
| Water | Contract / minimum line / hostage | Cutoff sanctions |
| Labor | Forced service / contract / civic service | The military-household register |
| Records | Closed / cross-verified / fully public | Wills and ledgers |
| Technology | House secret / licensed sharing / joint ledger | Pump and cooling keys |
| Trade | Blockade / licensed / open market | Auctions and convoys |
| Succession | Bloodline / adoptive apprenticeship / open examination | Stewards and elders |
| Bonding | Monogamy license / contract cohabitation / house bonding / military-register bonding / open bonding | Marriage and cohabitation ledgers. Organizations did not assign sexual taste |

Operating houses HC and HP and external theaters XT use the same fields. House records appear in the [world narrative atlas](/wiki/World-Narrative-Atlas/). Details by layer appear in [Houses](/wiki/Chaebol-Houses-and-Century-Factions/).

## The Three Factions' Practices (Opening)

**A. Meaning of the Indicators**

Values stood for the practice the current majority followed.

| Faction | Salient axes | Practice at the opening |
|---|---|---|
| Government | Authority (command), qualification (heredity) | Recognizes as citizens only those entered in its registers. Trades pass from parents to children. The President serves for life. |
| Free City | Decentralization (living-sphere partition) | Each city holds its own power; there is no king. Goods and skills are exchanged. |
| Arc | Authority (Hierophant and families) | Rule runs through families, and believers receive baptism. |
