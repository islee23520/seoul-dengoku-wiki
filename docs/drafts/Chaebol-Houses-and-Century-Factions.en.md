After the Great Blackout, scarcity in Seoul forced children to take up their parents' work. As inherited work piled up, a vertical hierarchy hardened, and the family became the unit that held that hierarchy in place. The three powers, however, differ in how a family passes on its position. In the Government, a seat holding a duchy passes to the next generation. In the Free City, skills and workshops pass down. In the Arc, a family's authority centred on the Hierophant passes down.

<a id="가문과-키의-분할"></a>

## Inherited Seats and Keys

<a id="가문과-키의-분할-p1"></a>
<a id="가문과-키의-분할-p2"></a>

The Government rules the 14 districts north of the Han River. In each district, the elected district representative becomes a Senator and holds that district's duchy. On paper, elections still exist. But under scarcity, only a handful of families can gather votes, so the seats have effectively become hereditary. The Government recognizes as citizens only those recorded in its own registers, so people outside the registers have almost no path to such a seat. The same thing happens further down. At Yongsan Station in Yongsan-gu, the Seollo family passes the keys of the rail transshipment facility to the next generation. The position held by the Seollo family is called Stationmaster as a provisional title, and its holder has not been confirmed. Who holds each district's Senator seat has not been decided either.

The Free City is a scattering of independent city-states across the 7 southwestern districts. It has no king, and since there is no way to judge whose skill is greater, it has not been able to choose a leader. Instead, skills and workshops are passed down within families. The data for the Duke of Gwanak, I Ilseop, still lists him as heir of the I family of the First Branch Workshop. For cities outside Gwanak, the title of duchy holder remains at the proposal stage as "differs by city." As provisional titles, the head of a city is called Duke and the head of a workshop is called Depot-Master.

Han Jae-mok of the Cheongju Han clan is recorded as representative of the water-contract families. In older records, the council of water-contract families was based at Yangpyeong Station (Yeongdeungpo-gu).

The Arc consists of the 4 Gangnam districts and split off from the Free City. It is ruled by the Hierophant Go Ye-jin, whom the faithful call the Messiah. Its rule is a Shia-style family rule. The faithful build up their faith through baptism, replacing parts of their bodies with machines. When they die, their brains are sealed and placed in the sanctuary to become memories of the First AI. The titles for the heads of the Seocho-gu, Gangnam-gu, Songpa-gu, and Gangdong-gu duchies are still at the proposal stage (provisional titles: Clan-Head for a clan unit, Keeper of the Machines for a station unit), and their holders have not been decided.

## The Government That Rules the City Centre

On paper, the Government maintains ministries representing the three branches of the Republic of Korea and a multiparty system. In reality, however, there are only two parties, the OMO Party and the Claw Party, and the actual system is a modern feudalism. The Senators gather candidates from among themselves and produce a President, who serves for life. The arrangement resembles the prince-elector system of the Holy Roman Empire, in which a few princes chose the emperor.

Because the seats that have hardened into hereditary holdings are the very foundation of power, that foundation is contested even within the Government. The duty-family factions clash over the lifetime presidency, the Records Office, the Government Complex Guard, and stamp duty revenue. Onminui, led by Kim Yeon-gyu, is one of these factions. Even while fighting internally, the Government treats the entire area south of the Han River as barbarians beyond the wall.

## Beyond Seoul

The land outside Seoul is not ruled by people. The AI did not kill humanity directly; it brought down winter. Under that winter, the war machines of the Great Blackout roam, still carrying out their last orders. The Imjin River Gateway Theater, West Sea Granary Theater, Korea Strait Theater, Tumen River Far East Theater, and Incheon Trusteeship Theater found in old records are not political powers but merely names in the records. As of the winter of 2126, the stage on which families compete for position is the three powers and the 25 duchies.
