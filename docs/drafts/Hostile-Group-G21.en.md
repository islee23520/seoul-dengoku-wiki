## Habitat record

Early-winter-night damp has soaked into the relay niche of the Eastern Command underground duct on the Gwangjin Line. The Eastern Command duct watch stands in front of the iron door. Inside the door a person holds the brow to the duct wall.

That person's mouth and neck are wet, and a gurgle comes from the throat. The brow touches the plate where the heating pipe runs. At each outgoing breath the brow strikes the plate.

The person toward the door strikes once with the brow at each breath. The person in the middle keeps both hands on the rivets and strikes at a short interval. The person toward the junction has both chest and brow flat against the duct wall.

The three people's chests face the joint plate. The heads are toward the plate where the knocking lingers. The Eastern Command duct watch's flashlight lights the rivet line of that plate.

Dueoksini is in the junction niche. The head is broader than the shoulders, and the brow presses the upper duct wall. The crown strikes the insulation of the ceiling pipe. The Wall-knockers outside stand along the wall that brow touches.

The Nail-scratcher stands in front of the plate at hand height. The nails are long and the tips are white. The nail tips are in the grooves between the rivets, and the scratched iron shines in the flashlight.

The Yagwanggwi stands in the dark duct outside the niche. Blue light comes from the chest and the backs of the hands. A Wall-knocker's face is inside that light, and the Amsa–Godeok merchant band's repair sack lies beside the light.

Waterproof tape and a spare latch from the relay box are in the Eastern Command duct watch's vest. Spare flashlight cells lie on the box lid. The shift slip of the Eastern Command watch and the Anguk Order watch is stuck to the box lid.

Hangangjin Bridge Council's lock hangs on the niche latch. A Transmission Relay mark and the Imjin River Gate Theater bypass number are written on the box lid. The Eastern Command duct watch sets the iron-door latch. The watch's helmet lies on the threshold with a crack in it.
