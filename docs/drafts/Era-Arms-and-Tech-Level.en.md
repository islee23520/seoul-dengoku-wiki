A. Status of the equipment classification

Seoul had trade tools, police equipment, and military equipment in 2026. After the Great Blackout, mechanics repaired surviving tools and recovered machine parts. Modern and future weapons, humanoids, and machines survive the Great Blackout. In an age of scarcity their numbers, ammunition, and parts run short, and they are dear.

<a id="한-세기가-지난-손"></a>

## Equipment Used Across a Century

A. Chronology and equipment

In the Great Blackout, around 2026, equipment that lost remote control was run by hand. The generation of 2126 repairs and uses tools and equipment inherited from that time.

B. Firearms regulation and remnants

The 2026 firearms law required permits for firearms and crossbows. K-series small arms also existed at the time.

C. Reuse of robot parts

After the Great Blackout, factory robots lost their call network. Mechanics repaired surviving hoses, terminals, and lenses.

## Arms and Units by Faction

A. What each faction holds

Arms follow the places where facilities, and the people who know how to use them, ended up scattered. No faction can use the weapons it holds fully on its own. The reasons are set out in [The Cost Structure of the Three Factions](/wiki/Cost-Structure/).

| Faction | Arms it holds | What it must get from others to use them fully | Starting units |
|---|---|---|---|
| Government (President Kim Yeongyu) | Service rifles (restored K2 pattern), squad machine guns, mortars, restored armored carriers and tanks, self-propelled guns, depots | Fire-control, drone and air-defense software (Arc); doctors who can send the badly wounded back (Free Cities) | Service rifle squad, patrol shield squad, officer command team, first-aid team |
| Free Cities (Chair of the Board I Ilseop) | Capital Defense Command stock rifles, machine guns, armored vehicles and tanks; maintenance tools; doctors and neural-link surgery | Officers to command tanks and heavy weapons together (Government); medical-robot software (Arc); original seed stock (Government) | Conscript rifle company, militia bayonet band, doctor-led aid team, maintenance engineers, rail handcar |
| Arc (Hierophant Go Ye-jin) | Guard rifles, command-input mechanical bodies, remote-steered insects, net launchers, shutdown signal emitters | Factories and depots to repair chassis (Government); neural-direct surgery that makes a body move by thought (Free City doctors) | Guard riflemen (the Eastern Command), mechanical-body devotees, insect scouts, net capture team |

B. Drones and surveillance

Drones and surveillance gear are not weapons anyone starts with; factions acquire them later. At the start no faction can fly a fighting drone. The Arc's remote-steered insects only scout. The Free Cities are first to build tethered scout drones, using optical fiber recovered from fields of unexploded warheads. Because they are steered by wire, the Arc cannot switch them off. The Arc nets roaming drone swarms, implants control modules and tames them. The Government restores military surveillance drones, but their flight control runs only under an Arc license.

C. Roaming machines and ancient weapons

The AI is not a common enemy of the three factions. A roaming machine is a hazard that falls on the buildings outside the shutters, and a resource that becomes a unit once it is caught, repaired and woken. Ancient weapons found now and then (an air-defense system, an armored locomotive) wake only when two or more factions put their skills together, so a find is itself a diplomatic event. Units, weapon codes and unlock conditions by tier are in [Unit Roster and Tech Tree](/wiki/Unit-Roster-and-Tech-Tree/).

## What Actually Existed in 2026

A. Scope of the reviewed sources

In 2026, permit rules for firearms and crossbows, military equipment, and railway and facility tools existed. Facility staff ran equipment by hand immediately after the Great Blackout.

- Firearms and Swords Act, Article 1: it governed the manufacture, sale, lease, transport, possession, and use of guns, swords, explosives, spray devices, electroshock weapons, and crossbows. This was not a country where civilians carried guns as daily routine. Effective dates and clause detail followed the statute's original text. ([Korea Law Service Center](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsSc.do?menuId=1&query=%EC%B4%9D%ED%8F%AC%C2%B7%EB%8F%84%EA%B2%80%C2%B7%ED%99%94%EC%95%BD%EB%A5%98), viewed 2026-09-12; earlier capture `.omo/ulw-research/20260911-200129/browse/01-law-go-kr-firearms-swords.png`)
- Public material introduces the K2 as the ROK Armed Forces' service rifle. Post-collapse possession through military remnants or market inflow differs from case to case. Who holds how many in 2026 was not what this source said. ([English Wikipedia, Daewoo K2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daewoo_Precision_Industries_K2), viewed 2026-09-12)
- A 2015 policy briefing stated that general reserve training issued M16s in place of carbines. The training issue fact of the time was confirmed, but 2026 counts, per-unit issue tables, and warehouse locations were not fixed by this material. ([Policy Briefing](https://www.korea.kr/briefing/policyBriefingView.do?newsId=148791733), viewed 2026-09-20)
- SNT Motiv's public defense page shows the existence of the K-series production line. The manufacturer's product descriptions did not stand in for actual issue counts to the armed forces or reserves. ([SNT Motiv](https://www.sntmotiv.com/eng/business/defense.html), viewed 2026-09-20)
- The original 2026 police equipment regulations and the issue range of service pistol models do not survive, and neither do the regulation numbers.
