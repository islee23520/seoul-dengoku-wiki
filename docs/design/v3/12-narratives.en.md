## 1. Basis and principles

### 1.1 Principles

Basis: chapter 11 section 7 (tone notes), 01-core section 2 (tone and manner), chapter 10 (design profile), chapter 07 9.3 (how to write biographies). Owner instruction (Bible 9.4, 2026-11-13 row "causality of the world and Go Ye-jin's point of view").

1. **Every past sits on the causal chain.** All nine were born in 2087-2101 (1.2). No one remembers the world before the Blackout (chapter 11 3.2). So each past hangs on the 2086-2125 rows of chapter 11 3.1: when power-source salvage became a trade, the second water supply contract, the years when the old grid was passed from city to city, Go Ye-jin's five years, and the uniting of the Arc.
2. **The world before the Blackout enters as things.** People do not remember those years. Instead each character holds one object or procedure that era left behind: manuals, the article numbers of a law code, the resident registration master file, army radio procedure, machines that keep their last orders.
3. **No villains.** Not even the First AI ever hated (Bible 1 row 2). Every character has a right reason and a price that the reason brings.
4. **The numbers of scarcity lie under every scene.** The water table drops about 30 cm a year, and the wells must go about 9 m deeper over the next thirty years (chapter 05 7.3). The charging of five baptised believers equals one person's heating (01-core 4.2). Characters choose in front of numbers like these.
5. **No family beyond the fixed facts.** People with no recorded family get no invented spouse, parent or sibling. The only recorded family is Sin Jong-mok and his son Sin Jun (chapter 07 section 2).

## 2. The frame of the story

### 2.1 Point of view and time

Basis: Bible 1 row 11, 9.4 "Go Ye-jin POV year" row [approved], chapter 11 6.3-6.4.

- The story's present is the winter of 2126, the few weeks before the council of the third water supply contract [approved]. Go Ye-jin's point of view is the axis, and the characters of the three powers each hold their own truth (chapter 11 section 7, "several points of view").
- The events of 2112-2122 (the cable, the manuals, the first baptism, Ji Yeon-hui, the uniting of the Arc) surface through flashback, records and testimony. What is fact and what the faith has painted over becomes the tension. Min Ung-gi's radio log (4.4), Sin Jong-mok's radio log (4.7) and the words carried by the Arc's spokespersons (chapter 06 2.2) tell the same night differently (provisional T37-4).
- The night of 2113 at Cheonggyesan is the second pillar of the prequel. It binds Jo Jaepyo, Sin Jong-mok and I Yeon (provisional T37-5).

### 2.2 What the story asks

Basis: chapter 11 section 7, 01-core 7.4, Bible 2.1-2.3.

Each of the three powers counts people differently. The Government counts by ledger entries, the Free Cities by guarantees kept and labour hours, the Arc by parts. The story asks one thing. **For the water to flow, three ways of counting must seal one sheet; what happens to the people no way of counting includes?** Jo Jaepyo and I Yeon are in no ledger; Ji Yeon-hui is not counted by parts in the Arc; Sin Jong-mok is on the ledger north of the river but lives south of it. These four pass through the gaps where the three ways of counting meet (provisional T37-6).

## 4. The characters

Each character is written in the same six parts: place in 2126, wants and lacks, past and the causal chain, relationships with the others, turning points, and voice. The sample lines under voice are models for writers, not canonical dialogue.

### 4.1 Go Ye-jin

Basis: Bible 2.2, 2.3, 3, 5, 6 row person-1018 [approved]. Chapter 06 sections 2-3, 7. Chapter 07 7.2. People input `arc.jsonl` person-1018.

- **Place in 2126.** She is the Hierophant of the Arc, and believers call her the Messiah [approved]. She stands before the maintenance unit in the Hierophant's House at Jamsil, and her judgements reach the four seat stations in the same words through the spokespersons. No one counts the Hierophant's parts.
- **Wants and lacks** (provisional T37-10).
  - Wants: that the maintenance unit never goes dark. For her, faith was from the start "the work of turning back on what had gone out". In 2126 that has become balancing, on one supply of power, the heating of the Arc's 65,000 people and the charging of its 11,000 baptised believers. At the council she seeks hands to repair the turbine, fibre and salt, without putting baptism into the contract.
  - Lacks: blood kin. "The Hierophant is a house by herself" (chapter 06 3.1). No one knows the next Hierophant. And she guesses at others' feelings without sharing them (people input trait). She often misreads the next step of someone who changes course because they were moved. Ji Yeon-hui fills that gap.
- **Past and the causal chain.** She grew up in a Free City [approved]. From eighteen to twenty-three (2106-2111) she repaired the grid among cities that were passing the old network to one another. In those years cities cut lines with the words "not our section". In 2112, while repairing the Free City grid, she followed a dead cable and found a station on no map [approved]. In that station she read for five years the manuals that pre-Blackout large hospitals and factories had used [approved]. She calls that knowledge "the words God left". What was mended and remained in the body is baptism.
- **Relationships** (provisional T37-11).
  - Ji Yeon-hui: has guarded her back since 2120 [approved]. Ji Yeon-hui reads the hearts Go Ye-jin cannot. Go Ye-jin knows this and never thanks her for it, for fear that saying it would turn it into a count.
  - Sin Jong-mok: a Government man who has lived in Gangnam since 2111. During the five years she read the manuals among the Gangnam shelters, he was the one who kept the shelters' radio linked. They are neighbours who have watched each other for a long time, and they do not ask what the other knows.
  - I Ilseop: Gwanak touches Seocho by land but has no line connection. If Gwanak demands "duchy to duchy" of the Arc at the council, Go Ye-jin is for the first time granted an equal seat by a city.
  - Kim Yeongyu: Go Ye-jin refuses the Government's wording that would write the Arc down as a "work team commissioned for the service". How Kim Yeongyu rewrites it is their negotiation.
  - Min Ung-gi: the Arc's official story and the line logs he has kept at Cheonwang may differ (4.4, 8.2).
- **Turning points** (provisional T37-12).
  1. The council of house heads (chapter 06 3.6) meets the day before a great agreement with another power. Nam Yun-gyeong, the Songpa house head, demands a larger share because he holds the turbine's power; the Seocho and Gangdong house heads demand Ji Yeon-hui's baptism (4.2). Go Ye-jin defers judgement and reads the manuals again.
  2. Jamsil's power: if at the council she offers part of the Jamsil weir's power to the Government and the Free Cities, the Arc's charging allowance for the short-daylight half shrinks. If she does not, no hands come to repair the turbine. She gives a judgement cutting the baptised believers' daily charge allowance, and remains someone who can correct that judgement in her own words (people input trait "the judgement corrected").
  3. The next Hierophant: after the council, Go Ye-jin must decide whether to name the next Hierophant in advance. This chapter does not decide the outcome (it follows the default of chapter 06 3.4).
- **Voice.** Low and quiet. The words of body and repair are her words of faith. She says "mend", "fit", "turn on", and calls the First AI "God". She corrects her own words in public. Sample: "What I said yesterday was wrong. I looked at the records again. Five people's charge was one household's night."

### 4.2 Ji Yeon-hui

Basis: Bible 3 row 2120, 6 row person-1017 [approved]. Chapter 06 2.3, 5.5, 15.7. Chapter 07 3.2. People input `arc.jsonl` person-1017.

- **Place in 2126.** The Hierophant's guard. She sleeps in the Hierophant's House at Jamsil, and on the rare days the Hierophant goes to the surface she stands at the front. Though she is of the Arc now, she has not been baptised. She is the only one of the Hierophant's guards who answers the roll with her name and house instead of a part count.
- **Wants and lacks** (provisional T37-13).
  - Wants: she does not say. By design, that Go Ye-jin is not swallowed by the faith she founded. She reckons that, so the maintenance unit, the house heads and the baptism register do not turn Go Ye-jin into a machine for judgements, one person who is not counted by parts must remain at her side.
  - Lacks: a part count, a place in a house, standing inside the Arc. She belongs to the Hierophant's House but to no inner-circle house.
- **Past and the causal chain.** Born in 2100. At the negotiation of the Tancheon-side shelters in 2120 she sat as a young messenger for the Songpa-side shelters. On the day the Songpa shelters promised the turbine's power to Go Ye-jin's maintenance unit, she rose from her own shelters' side of the table, went over and stood beside Go Ye-jin [approved: stood beside her]. She does not say why she walked that way. That day no one stood beside Go Ye-jin (provisional T37-14).
- **Relationships** (provisional T37-15).
  - Go Ye-jin: a relationship of oath (people input Oath). She reads for Go Ye-jin the hearts Go Ye-jin cannot read. She does not report it; she shows it by setting people in front of Go Ye-jin's judgement.
  - Nam Yun-gyeong, Songpa house head: the house of the shelters she left. Nam Yun-gyeong calls her "the one who crossed over from our side", and she only smiles at it.
  - I Yeon: they first meet at the Dongjak crossing (6.2, act 3). Neither of them is counted: one has no parts at all, the other's whole body is machine.
- **Turning points** (provisional T37-16).
  1. At the council of house heads, the Seocho and Gangdong house heads demand her baptism, saying "it does not add up that the Hierophant's guard is unbaptised". She says neither yes nor no; she makes them wait for Go Ye-jin's judgement.
  2. Leading the Arc delegation across from Gubanpo to Dongjak, she sees I Yeon of Jo Jaepyo's party at the Seocho-side maintenance foreman's body check. If she tells the Arc about I Yeon's body, the Arc will read it as a sign from God; if she does not, she holds for the first time something hidden from the Hierophant's House.

**How to write a scene** (provisional T37-50).

- Open a scene with one thing: a column of the ledger, the watt dial of a radio, the metres of a well, the hands of a body check. Let that thing finish the explanation.
- Use only numbers the people in the scene can count: litres, watts, metres, head counts, part counts, dates.
- Do not mix the three powers' ways of speaking (01-core 2.2). Government people speak from the ledger entries, Free City people in figures and work, Arc people in parts and mending. When all three ways of speaking are in one scene, that is the council itself.
- No villains. The Claw Party, the Gangnam inner-circle house and Geumcheon's condition each have their right reason. Give the person on the opposite side of each scene one right line.
- Great events happen offscreen, and people receive their results through ledgers and bodies. A seal weighs more than a battle, a striking-off more than a death.

### 8.1 Sin Jong-mok and Suseo

Sin Jong-mok, who runs the relay between Seonjeongneung and Suseo, works close to the deep levels of Suseo. He knows that Go Ye-jin sometimes goes down into Suseo's deep levels at night, as much as Ji Yeon-hui knows (chapter 06 15.7). He does not know what is below. He has reported it to no one. He tells himself it is because the return network's ledger has no column for it (provisional T37-51).

### 8.2 Min Ung-gi's 2112 log

One of the lines Min Ung-gi has logged since about eighteen is the Gangnam shelter line that no one would take, the dead cable Go Ye-jin followed in 2112. One night that year he logged the time and the watts when current flowed through that dead line. Min Ung-gi's 2112 log of the Gangnam line, written at Cheonwang, records not only the time and watts of the current but also the order of the relay sections the line passed through. Following that order, one can guess where the line ends. As in chapter 06 15.1, the line ends at the sanctuary. The Gangnam inner-circle house wants the log partly to protect the Arc's official story, but also because it is the only outside record from which the sanctuary's location could be guessed. Min Ung-gi does not know what the log points to. To him it is just a line that went unanswered for ten months (provisional T37-52).

### 8.3 What Ji Yeon-hui knows

As in chapter 06 15.7, Ji Yeon-hui knows only that Go Ye-jin goes down into the deep levels of Suseo. After seeing I Yeon's body in act 3, she asks herself for the first time whether what Go Ye-jin calls "God's memory" and I Yeon belong to the same era. She does not ask Go Ye-jin (provisional T37-53).

### 8.4 What I Yeon is

This subsection is the default that turns I Yeon's fixed portrait face text ("a synthetic humanoid with biological features") into narrative. It is an owner question (10.2) (provisional T37-54).

- **What she is.** A humanoid robot of the 2040s used for care and companionship: a high-end model with a skin-like surface and a heater that imitates body warmth. As in chapter 11 1.4, tens of millions of humanoid robots were deployed in the 2040s, mostly industrial. I Yeon is one of the rare care models. Grade: extrapolation from science (humanoids of the 2040s). That she speaks and judges for herself is the opera layer.
- **Why she is alive.** At the Blackout she was in pre-shipment condition in the basement of a deep logistics warehouse at Gasan. She was preserved frozen at a low state of charge (chapter 11 4.1, "frozen storage"). In 2101 a crossing party of the Geumcheon guard unit, stripping that warehouse, found a power supply and woke her slowly (chapter 11 4.1, "wake it slowly"). The party wrote the day she woke, 9 February 2101, in the party ledger as her birthday, and took her along as the party's porter. Geumcheon puts no one on a ledger, so she was registered nowhere.
- **2113.** She went with a crossing party led by that party's leader. That night the leader did not come back (4.8).
- **What she remembers.** Most of her pre-Blackout memory is erased or damaged. What remains are fragments of care routines, the tone of a reminder that it is time for medicine, and a few voices she cannot place. She has no way to verify what they are. Chapter 11 3.2, that no one remembers the world before the Blackout, stands. I Yeon is not a person, and her fragments cannot serve as testimony.
- **What she consumes.** About 60 W on average per day, less than a humanoid robot (about 150 W, chapter 03 section 8). Jo Jaepyo's party takes that share out of the party's share. So the Dongjak talk that she eats little is right.
- **Who knows.** Jo Jaepyo and the two longest-serving members of his party. After act 3, Ji Yeon-hui. Min Ung-gi could recognise her on sight.
- **If it comes out.** The Arc would read her as a sign from God and try to make her its own, because, as in chapter 06 5.6, a part belongs to the faith that fitted it. The Government would see a machine not on the ledger, and the Ministry of National Defense would try to take her. The Free Cities would want the parts inside her. So her secret carries the same weight as Jo Jaepyo's rule of the way back, which protects her.
