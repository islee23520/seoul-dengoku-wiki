# The Faith-and-Culture Schism

The night the generation of 2126 memorized as the first line of the creation story was the Great Blackout. The canon name was [motive-power revocation](../overview/World-Unbinding.md), and the year 2026. The story's present was 2126. The institutional and religious changes of the century between were written year by year in [the Hundred-Year Annals of Seoul Dengoku](../chronology/Century-Annals.md).

Faith did not arise because a stationmaster or a Moderator proclaimed it. It grew on the night the announcements went silent, on the morning the sluices opened, in the person who died before the platform's yellow line. Occupying a gu did not change temperament.

In the Korea of 2026 there had been no religious majority. Pew Research 2010 wrote 46% non-religious, 29% Christian, 23% Buddhist ([Pew](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2014/08/12/6-facts-about-christianity-in-south-korea/), viewed 2026-09-17). The lack of a majority vote did not mean the institutions were weak. The pulpits, altars, General Directorate, and chapels crossed the century on real estate, registers, relief, and calendars, and the watchkeepers' grandchild's grandchild was the head of the time. Living pastors, church logos, and presbytery arms were not carried onto opening-day persons. The state names Sermon Registry State, Cathedral Accreditation State, Sangha Relief State, and Won Buddhism were not erased.

## A Century Hardened the States

The Seoul of 2026 was still the capital of one country. The Military Manpower Administration took volunteer intake selection; the conglomerate families controlled the affiliates with small stakes; the pulpits, altars, General Directorate, and chapels held real estate and registers. Water, rail, markets, medicine, and communications ran on one call mesh of institutional and family ledgers. The hands that later became states grew from that year's watch rosters.

On the night of the revocation the fleet calls, GNSS, the mobile networks, and the licensing servers stopped waiting for one another. The grid SCADA, belonging to the same mesh, folded the lights. The oral record kept that night alone as the Great Blackout. The Yeouido plant, the Yeonji-dong General Assembly, the Myeongdong altar, the Jogyesa General Directorate, the Heukseok chapel, and the Gwanghwamun complex exercised emergency powers to save their own jurisdictions. The joint command mesh broke that night. A century later the Yeouido hall had split off as a neighboring state, and the Yeongdeungpo Station sluice house sold the western water. When the powers were returned stayed the seed of a century of legitimacy disputes.

The clock on which manufacture died and the clock on which society changed ran separately. The fabs stopped within a few years, but for offices to become ranks, the generation that remembered the shell of the corporation that had turned the fabs had to die. The children who received the keys still used the words "company," "General Assembly," and "diocese"; their grandchildren read the keys as the family's signature. When the grandchild's grandchild sat in the head-of-state seat, the emergency committee had become the creation oral record and the watch roster a charter. That gap was the hundred years. The canon of the calendar was in [the Scenario Timeline](../chronology/Scenario-Timeline.md).

The titles of the creation oral record remained, with no common-era years attached. In the First Water Compact the hands that read it as public rights and the hands that read it as a protection contract split. In the military-household register dispute the hands that demanded household-unit service registration and the hands that refused split. The Sixteen Banners rose, the Merit Subjects' Winter scattered or hardened the titles, and the Second Water Compact wrote the minimum line, the standards, and the emergency convoys again. A founder entered in the ledgers and a person seated at the opening were not read as one body.

On opening day, the central water-and-parts contract handed down from the creation oral record expired. Moderator Im Hajun of the Sermon Registry State was already gone. O Gyeongjae took the Moderator's seat through General Assembly accreditation, and the adopted heir Im Chowon remained in the teacher's seat holding half the hammer. The price of water rose, medical convoys ran late, and the lesser states' overlapping contracts began. Faith did not rewrite that season's script; it locked only the fact that water, registers, and rites were one fight.

The main stage was the Seoul subway. The capitals were subway stations. Yeongdeungpo, Guro, and Yangjae were not state names but the stations of the capitals. The list and origins of the sixteen states were canon in [the Sixteen States of Seoul](../factions/Sixteen-States.md).

## The Four Theocracies

The theocracies were the form of government of four of the sixteen states. They were not the state religion of all Seoul. The titles kept the words those institutions used in 2026, and the tiers followed the five layers of [Offices and Ranks](../offices/Offices-and-Ranks.md). No living pastor, sitting bishop, or order logo was moved onto an opening-day person. Those seated at the opening were the 2126 generation of [the Characters](../characters/Core-Characters.md).

### Sermon Registry State

In 2026 the General Assembly of the Sermon Registry State held registers, relief, and church real estate at the General Assembly Hall, 29 Daehak-ro 3-gil, Yeonji-dong, Jongno-gu. The 29% Christian of Pew 2010 was no majority, and the majority within it was Protestant. Weak at the ballot, the pulpits were strong in real estate.

On the night of the revocation the General Assembly registers became the watch roster. When the announcements went silent, the churches' emergency lights became the watch's fire. A century later the surviving pulpit real estate lay in Gangnam, so the capital of the time was Samsung Station. The state name stood as the General Assembly's own name.

The Moderator of the time was O Gyeongjae. Im Hajun descended from the surety line of the Second Water Compact in the creation oral record but did not overlap that body. Before vanishing he left three wills and sealed the original chest of the pump-cleaning process. Im Chowon was the adopted heir who received half the hammer, and her rank was teacher. O Gyeongjae held the other half — General Assembly accreditation — and sat in the Moderator's seat. The trade aliases "pump-technology steward" and "treatment-principle transmitter" did not go into the ledger cells. The cell's title was Moderator.

The public faith was the Pump-Workshop Rite. It bound pump and workshop into one rite and read the hand that brought water as a blessing. When Han Jaemok, stationmaster of the Water Compact, read the same tap as contract, the Samsung Station pulpit read that tap as blessing. The House-Secret Rite lay inside and traveled with the succession dispute. Ttukseom was not the capital; the drawings of the Ttukseom Heat-Source House remained only a house rite on unclaimed land.

What was not brought in: living pastors' real names, church logos, presbytery names, Yoido Full Gospel Church. Lineage names grown from subway wreckage stayed fiction, and the state name was not erased.

### Cathedral Accreditation State

In 2026 the Cathedral Accreditation State held the altar and diocesan registers of Myeongdong Cathedral, 74 Myeongdong-gil, Jung-gu. Accreditation of marriage, adoption, and memorial rites came from that altar.

After the revocation the altar moved memorial and marriage accreditation onto paper registers. A century on, the real estate and the order of relief remained. The diocese of the time was a state accrediting marriages and memorial rites at Myeongdong Station. The Bishops' Conference building on Myeonmok-ro, Gwangjin, remained only an outer warehouse of the diocese.

The archbishop of the time was Nam Yungyeong. The title was archbishop; the trade alias was auction mediator. The hand that had kept the scales of the Seoul Station Inventory House unbroken sat in that seat. Garak's price hub stood empty as unclaimed land, and the archdiocese could not feed that empty ledger.

The public faith was the Auction-Disclosure Rite. When the bell rang, that day's justice was set. The bell of the distribution-and-consumption lineage overlapped here with the theocracy's altar. A station that took down its altar became a blank in the creation story.

What was not brought in: sitting bishops' real names and diocesan arms.

### Sangha Relief State

In 2026 the Sangha Relief State held the memorial tablets, temple rolls, and rite calendar at Jogyesa and the General Directorate, 55 Ujeongguk-ro, Jongno-gu. The 23% Buddhist of Pew was not a 2126 vote. The reason the order's slot remained was the General Directorate's watch.

After the revocation the General Directorate kept the tablets and the temple rolls. The capital of the time was Anguk Station. The state name stood as the order's own name. Other orders like Taego and Cheontae were not opened as a seventeenth state.

The supreme patriarch of the time was Baek On. The title was supreme patriarch; the trade alias was refugee delegate. The hand that had kept the register chests of the Bukhansan Bogukmun Return Society unbroken sat in that seat. The Bukhansan refuge was not a Hu but a vacancy, used as no state name. The lodging rite remained in the register chests beside the Anguk tablets.

The public faith was the lodging oath. The habit of giving lodging to those crossing the mountain had hardened into the order's teaching. The tablets and relief were the Jogye Order's sovereignty. Medicine was sold by the Jegi-dong Medical Neutrality League. Though the windows overlapped, the state name was not divided.

What was not brought in: splitting particular head temples into other state names, and raising the refugee vacancy into one of the sixteen slots.

After the revocation the Heukseok dharma-officer watch transcribed the charter. A century later Heukseok Station was Won Buddhism's Seoul capital. Seats were not counted by Pew shares. Where the watch remained, there was the state.

The public faith was the Settlement-Right Rite. Ice and short-term stock were credit. When the power cut, the beep died; the doctrine died with it. The main lineage was the ledger lineage. Numbers and charter lay in one ledger.

What was not brought in: bringing the Iksan headquarters in as a Seoul state, and reviving the scrapped Noryangjin market as a capital.

## The Everyday That Grew in Stations

The units that collided on the map were not state names. In the Seoul of later days the routes, the gu, and the stations held identity power. The sixteen territory slots were kept. A slot was no flat partition but a living sphere binding treatment plants, depots, head offices, assemblies, dioceses, orders, transfers, and mountain-refuge corridors.

The station chapels remained as concourse notice boards, yellow lines, barrier gates, and ad frames; the gu chapels as government-complex entrances and ration windows. The route chapels stood as the milestones of depots and main-line tunnels. Injection from above failed. Even when Han Jaemok forced the Water-Compact Rite on the western lesser states, the Cheonwang depot's engineers held their bolt rites first. Even when Bae Ujin planted the Protected-Water Rite in the east, Jegi-dong applied the treatment principle to friend and foe alike.

The Sinjeong depot district lined up before the ration taps instead of reciting with the sluice guards. In the Cheonwang depot district the order of closing the barrier gates was the prayer. Within the same west, a different station saw different taboos. The southeast did not look like one lump. The Gangnam cooling key, the Samsung pulpit, the Yangjae depot, and the Heukseok charter were neighbors that touched different things before the meal.

Faith divided into three strata. The upper layer was not overturned; the lower was mended.

| Stratum | What it binds | Who names it | Is it the rule-changing unit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lineage | Families raising the same wreckage · the same symbols into godhead | Set by the living sphere. Nearly unchanging | No. A row of the matrix |
| Faith | Named communities. Chapels and priests | Moderators · archbishops · supreme patriarchs · Kyujeongwon heads · stationmasters · priests · guilds | Occasionally. On merger and schism |
| Doctrine · teaching | Taboo, rite, succession qualification, oath wording | Factions and heresy trials | Yes. The basic editing unit |

Even within one route-map lineage, a faith that read transfer as salvation fought a faith that read only the terminal as rest. That was sectarianism. When one line of doctrine changed, marriage, adoption, vassal oath, ration priority, and grounds of legitimacy changed with it. The sixteen state slots were not increased. The faith slots grew.

Priests were a relation class of [persons, powers, trades](../characters/Characters-Factions-and-Professions.md). Priest and apprentice already stood in the succession paths. Tier 1 of the four theocracies was a layer of the land. Moderator, archbishop, supreme patriarch, and Kyujeongwon head were not trade aliases but ledger titles. This document laid doctrine over those paths.

The main lineage and the public faith need not match. The Yeouido Investment Union was ledger-lineage but its public rite was the On-Time-Broadcast Rite. Won Buddhism was ledger-lineage but its public rite was the Settlement-Right Rite. The Sermon Registry State was pulpit-lineage but its public rite was the Pump-Workshop Rite.

The pulpit, altar, and monastery lineages were left by institutions. The eight below were creative lineages grown from subway wreckage. The starting list was locked. They could later merge or split, but state names were not changed into wreckage-lineage names.

### Wreckage Was Not a Manual but a Rite

Severed equipment was not a manual but a rite bowl. When power came, a blessing had been received; when the announcement sounded, the word was fact. A name in the military register meant one could still be mustered.

What was worshipped was facilities and parts. No living king was raised into godhead first. The ruler held the keys of the facility and borrowed legitimacy from it.

Acts of piety were three: retrieval that moved wreckage into chapels, pilgrimage that circled sealed zones on schedule, and offering that set mended parts on the altar. Recorded as plunder, chapel authority was shaved.

The default of the pilgrimage route was the depots and plants people kept. The Sinjeong, Cheonwang, Godeok, and Sinnae depots were tied to the capitals of the time. Banghwa, Gunja, Changdong, and Suseo were unclaimed or edge corridors. When forced rites ran two seasons, hidden chapels arose.

### Consumption Rites

Old currency, distribution networks, and advertising became after the collapse not price tags but rites. The lineage name of this axis was the distribution-and-consumption lineage. It split from the ledger lineage. The ledger lineage was notarization and accreditation; the distribution-and-consumption lineage was banknotes, frames, and distribution paths. The five below were not the state-faith list of the time. They were factions within the distribution-and-consumption lineage.

The Banknote-Portrait Rite pasted the faces of old banknotes on chapel walls. There were stations that floated them on water to divine the ration order. They counted at Garak's empty windows and in the Noryangjin wreckage. Garak was not a state; Noryangjin was not a capital.

The Ad-Frame Rite held the platform advertisement as the portrait of a saint. Where the model's name was unknown, it was called only "the Honored Frame." The Euljiro concourse of Line 2 was the wall of the Yeouido Investment Union's retainer window. The Jamsil frame wall was the far end of the emptied sphere. Tear a frame out to light a fire, and that station's passage remained unclean for a season.

The Barcode Rite made the sound of the scan a license. There were priests who mimed the beep of dead terminals. It counted in the Dongdaemun night labor and the convenience-store wreckage. Dongdaemun was the Hu on the Medical Neutrality League's west. To take a thing without the sound was not theft but apostasy.

The Auction-Disclosure Rite was the Cathedral Accreditation State's public teaching. When the bell rang, that day's justice was set. It meshed with Nam Yungyeong's scales. Garak's empty ledger could not replace that bell.

The Settlement-Right Rite was Won Buddhism's. Ice and short-term stock were credit. When the power cut, the beep died; the doctrine died with it.

The hidden faction was the Dual-Price Rite. The dual ledgers of Daerim and Garibong attached here. Daerim was the Hu inside the Standards Alliance. A state that did not read the Korean-language price and the other-tongue price as one rite issued a seizure order.

The pilgrimage of the consumption rites was the distribution path — walked from wholesale to retail, from frame wall to checkout. When the path was blockaded, this lineage's chapel authority was shaved first. The open head temples were the Myeongdong altar and the Heukseok chapel. Suseo's empty depot was no priest to escort this rite; Jeong Yura merely leased the Suseo unclaimed zone's drawing chests.

### Machine Worship

The hand that mended machines was a rite before citizenship. This axis was no single lineage; the route-map, distribution, and fabrication lineages shared one piety.

The bodies counted as four that were touched into godhead.

The rolling-stock car body came first. The departure bell and the door-closing warning remained as prayers. The hand that greased the wheels remained as a remnant rite of the Changdong unclaimed zone, and the barrier-gate key was held by the Changdong Protection House of I Hongwon. Changdong was not a capital.

The substation room came second. The order of raising the breakers was read as a rite, and the distribution watch of the lamp-lighting rite stood as priest.

The lift machinery came third. When the elevator doors opened, passage was permitted, and the steps of the dead escalator remained a pilgrimage stair. It counted in the transfer stations where concourse heights differed.

The factory line came fourth. The conveyors and barrier gates of Guro and Geumcheon remained the body of the law; where the Open-Standards Rite spoke of the bolt, machine worship spoke of the machine the bit into.

When a repair succeeded, a restart ran, and a part was offered to the chapel, the maintenance house's mouth opened. When a restart failed, the maintenance house lost the standing to mend doctrine. Conversely, when a stopped train rolled again, that house could table a doctrine-revision bill for a season. That was this axis's path of reform.

The lamps, roars, and flat shells of the sealed underground lines were the extreme of machine worship. The chapel that saw them as saints and the chapel that saw them as plague split. No one wrote that the control code was deciphered.

### The Eight Wreckage Lineages

#### Route-map lineage

The colors and dots of the route map were scripture. The rolling stock was a moving shrine. This lineage took the car-body cell of machine worship.

The Transfer Pact counted at the Neutral Convoy City and the Line Succession State. Whoever transferred, even an enemy, the blades stopped. Terminal Rest overlapped the Wheel-Mobility Rite of the Seocho Computing Group. There were hands that read forcing a train past its terminus as apostasy. That the Jeongdong General Labor Union pushing trains beyond Suseo read as apostasy was a residual teaching left by the leased drawings over the emptied depot. Suseo was not a capital.

The hidden faction rode one color only. It was called the Route-Color Faction. The open station service held it a superstition, but the dispatch houses would not load freight into cars of another color.

#### Sluice lineage

The gate that gave water was the altar. The Arisu mark attached as an old-world symbol.

The Water Compact used the Water-Compact Rite close to official faith. Stationmaster Han Jaemok's legitimacy was unbroken water. The trade alias Water Director-General was no cell of the ledger. The Sermon Registry State's Pump-Workshop Rite bound pump and workshop into one rite. The Escort Protection State held the Protected-Water Rite: water given in exchange for escort. The Gate Military Government had only Guui's limited supply and held on with the Emergency-Water Rite.

When the supply cut, doctrine cracked first. The rites collapsed faster than the state grades.

#### Distribution lineage

When the light came on, words carried; when it went out, oaths were void — so believed some stations. It took the substation cell of machine worship.

In the lamp-lighting rite the switchgear watch was priest. The Capacitor-Relic Faction touched modules with charge left. It was no public faith. The Yangjae Machineworks Company saw it as a verification subject, and the chapels saw it as bone not to be touched. The Yangjae depot raised no state faith.

#### Fabrication lineage

The bolt standard was the law. It took the factory-line cell of machine worship.

The Open-Standards Rite of the Standards Alliance taught that parts anyone could use were citizenship. It meshed with Gang Minseo's repair-standard agenda. Ttukseom was unclaimed; the pumps and bolts had passed into the Standards Alliance's standards, and the House-Secret Rite of the Ttukseom Heat-Source House remained a house rite that posted no drawings to the council. The Seocho Computing Group held the Wheel-Mobility Rite: the hand that rolled a stopped train again was the rite.

The Guro dormitory remnants believed repair labor stood ahead of citizenship. They sat inside the fabrication lineage but took no council seal. The Daerim dormitories were the Hu inside this state.

#### Announcement-voice lineage

They fought over whether the word from the speaker was fact.

The On-Time-Broadcast Rite of the Yeouido Investment Union held the programming schedule as prayer. When Choi Jiwoo's cross-verification hardened into doctrine, official announcements waited on the Yeouido schedule. The Sangam transmission was an abandoned axis, not the capital of cross-verification. When trust broke once, the transmission towers became propaganda tools, and then the Silent Faction grew. It turned off the broadcast and trusted paper only.

The platform announcement was this lineage's terminal rite. A stop at which "this station" was never called remained a ghost stop. The wreckage of dead home-shopping voices mixed with the distribution-and-consumption lineage as a hidden rite.

#### Mobilization-register lineage

The sanctuary was where the pre-collapse conscription ledgers lay split. It made no new state. It laid rites over [conscription remnants and the military registers](../factions/Conscription-Remnants.md).

The Muster-Remnant Rite read names, service numbers, and the last muster date. It said the state could still muster. There was no such state. The Key-Drill Rite let only the armory watch touch the drill. Desertion was recorded as apostasy. A squad carrying issue firearms stood out — one reason doctrine kept them out of the drifter's starting gear.

When the Republic of Korea Government brought these registers into the Gwanghwamun archives, the ceremonial guard looked like a barracks. The Record-Accreditation Rite rose into heresy dispute, and the Records Bureau's legitimacy broke.

#### Ledger lineage

Numbers and seals were the oracle. This lineage kept the plaque, clock, and state-seal memory of the old-world symbols.

The Record-Accreditation Rite of the Republic of Korea Government saw succession and treaties as void without accreditation. It overlapped Yun Seorin's ambition. The Jeongdong General Labor Union held the Contract-Mediation Rite; the hand that recorded breaches and compensated losses was priest. The Yeouido Investment Union and Won Buddhism were also ledger-lineage; only their public rites split into the programming schedule and the checkout beep.

The ledger lineage did not raise prices into godhead. Price was the distribution-and-consumption lineage's business. When the two lineages shared one window, both seal and bell had to ring for the trade to close.

#### Distribution-and-consumption lineage

The lineage of the consumption-rite axis. The Banknote-Portrait Rite, Ad-Frame Rite, Barcode Rite, Auction-Disclosure Rite, and Settlement-Right Rite belonged here.

Myeongdong and Heukseok remained the open head temples; the Euljiro frame wall and the Dongdaemun night labor stood at the far ends. Garak, Jamsil, and Suseo were emptied spheres.

## Mixed, It Hid; Forced, It Split

The public faith was posted on the station notice board. Hidden faith sat in switchgear rooms, dormitories, the mouths of sealed galleries, behind frames, in the dual-ledger drawers.

Even when the state set one public rite, the station crowds kept a mixed practice. Hidden faith was a pressure valve. When a great power forced public rites, residents went down into the Capacitor-Relic Faction, the Silent Faction, the Dual-Price Rite, or the Route-Color Faction. On discovery, the grievance and reputation of [Powers and Diplomacy](../factions/Factions-and-Diplomacy.md) moved. Isolation weakened only outside promises; it did not automatically shave the local chapel's legitimacy.

The hidden rites of the corridors brought in no foreign denomination proper names. The habit of taking the interpretation fee first, and the habit of not torquing a bolt without a Vietnamese-language token attached, became teachings. This ban did not mean erasing the state names Sermon Registry State, Cathedral Accreditation State, Sangha Relief State, and Won Buddhism.

Water split before the same tap. Han Jaemok read the lock gate as contract; O Gyeongjae read the pump as blessing. The Myeongdong Auction-Disclosure Rite, the Heukseok Settlement-Right Rite, and Garak's empty ledger fought over one price. The Gwanghwamun Record-Accreditation Rite and the Yeouido On-Time-Broadcast Rite fought over which mouth was fact. When the Amsa Protected-Water Rite tried to bind military rations, the Jegi-dong treatment principle placed the sickbed outside the state. The Guui Emergency-Water Rite and the Achasan ridge almost merged into one military government, but did not. It was no condominium. Bring the armory registers into the Gwanghwamun archives, and the mobilization-register lineage's heresy dispute opened.

When forced rites ran two seasons, a hidden cell grew beside the public one. When two stations of one line were severed for a month, even within one lineage the doctrine slot split in two. Mixing was when one person kept two lineages together — the sluice guards held the Water-Compact Rite in public and, with it, the platform taboo of never stepping on the yellow line. A heretic was the side that had changed doctrine inside the same lineage. The Open-Standards Rite called the House-Secret Rite heretical; the Ttukseom Heat-Source House called it the other way.

Culture was no state ID. The naming keys remained the spheres. The `CultureKey` of [the heir, name-roster, and world ledger](../characters/Heirs-Names-and-World-Ledger.md) was a naming bundle. The state-key pairing of the 2126 opening kept [the Sixteen States of Seoul](../factions/Sixteen-States.md) as canon. Even when a state was annexed, the key remained.

The northern refugee key `north-refuge` folded its slot row for want of a capital. It only thickened the hidden cells of lodging, the Changdong wheel remnants, and the register chests; it became no seventeenth state. The matrix cells read as lineage × living culture. Cell values were four: public, minority, hidden, heresy-dispute.

The pulpit, altar, and monastery lineages were no rows of this table; they were read in the main-lineage cells of the sixteen-states table. Public remained notice-board rite; minority was kept by guilds alone. Hidden sat in switchgear rooms, dormitories, behind frames. Heresy dispute meant doctrine had already split within one lineage and trial was pending. The mobilization-register cell of the Gwanghwamun archives was that cell.

The culture kit bundled temperament, tradition, and service custom. Service custom was what the organizations keeping a facility held sacred.

Within one key, each station split again. The ration queues of Yeongdeungpo and the night shifts of Guro differed in collars, curses, and the order of rites. The Yeouido schedule was not the prayer of western water but of the investment ledgers. No unified culture was assumed.

The houses that kept generational names were those with ledgers — the Records Bureau, the sluice houses, the merchant houses. The rules were in [clan seats and generational names](../characters/Hangnyeol-and-Bon-gwan.md). Drifters and dormitory labor hardly kept them.

The migrant corridors were no seventeenth state. They lay over the living spheres as in [Diaspora Corridors](../factions/Diaspora-Corridors.md). When the habit of refusing contracts without interpretation hardened into temperament, that alley's culture kit counted apart from the homeland key. Names were not changed. Corridor cells added no row to the matrix; they thickened the hidden cells of the spheres they lay upon.

## The Cells Doctrine Changes

Doctrinal editing tied to half of story politics. Dynasties, vassals, titles, and conspiracies were rolled by [Ambitions](../characters/Ambitions-and-Relations.md). Faith judged whether those decisions were lawful.

The cells it touched were set in the table below.

| Cell doctrine touches | Effect |
|---|---|
| Succession qualification | Bloodline only, adoption allowed, priests only, technical examination only. Theocracies put laying-on-of-hands, General Assembly accreditation, and altar accreditation in front |
| Marriage · adoption into the register | Other lineages banned, same line only, corridor dual names allowed |
| Vassal oath | Which announcement, seal, tap, or ad frame is cited |
| Title holding | A heretic cannot be stationmaster. A hidden priest only deputizes. Theocratic tier 1 is a layer of the land |
| Ration priority | Rite-participating households first, or public auction only |
| Ground of legitimacy | Which of water, power, safety, legitimacy, technology, medicine, trade is locked or opened. The formula is written by [Powers and Diplomacy](../factions/Factions-and-Diplomacy.md) |
| Treaty accreditation | Which is mandatory — the Gwanghwamun seal, Yeouido schedule cross-verification, or City Hall contract mediation |
| Matrix cells | Public turned hidden, minority turned heresy |

An impossible promise did not pass by doctrine either. It was the same as the delivery check in the diplomacy document. A state without water that claimed the high priesthood of the Water-Compact Rite shaved only its local legitimacy.

When a successor changed, personal and institutional promises were separated. If an institutional promise carried doctrinal accreditation, the new successor had to hold that rite again. Without it, the vassals withheld their oaths.

A faction was a new faith slot under the same lineage. When two or more doctrines clashed, a priest named it, and the chapel split in two, a slot was born. At the opening, hidden-faith occupancy was a minority of the sphere's population. When forced rites ran two seasons, hidden occupancy grew.

At a station where sluice-lineage doctrine was strong, holding out an electricity ground alone won low approval. In a sphere where the distribution-and-consumption lineage was public, holding out a plaque alone without ringing the auction bell closed no trade.

When a lesser state became a great power, faith did not necessarily follow. Even if the Seocho Computing Group gripped the Changdong unclaimed zone's barrier gates tighter, the Wheel-Mobility Rite remained. Conversely, if the Water Compact lost its water, the Water-Compact Rite rose into heresy trial first. The Gangbuk treatment center lay outside the city in Namyangju, so northern bulk treatment did not exist at the opening. Northern water self-sufficiency was not a fact of the time but the story's counter-assumption.

## What Not to Put In

- Moving living pastors or sitting bishops onto opening persons. Using church logos, presbytery names, Yoido Full Gospel Church, or scripture titles verbatim in 2026 sentences
- Erasing the state names Sermon Registry State, Cathedral Accreditation State, Sangha Relief State, and Won Buddhism and renaming them after wreckage lineages
- A state faith covering all Seoul. Later Seoul was regionally split by default. The four theocracies were four of sixteen
- Keeping only dynastic vassalage and demoting wreckage rites and institutional succession to background sentences
- Top-down missionary success. Ignition came only from stations and trades
- A single muster reviving the old state. The register line was remnant, not reconstruction
- Unrolling lostech chassis into personal weapon lists to replace faith
- Applying combat grids or screen rules to this page
- Locking the culture key to the state ID
- Raising Garak, Suseo, Changdong, Sangam, Ttukseom, or Bukhansan Bogukmun to capitals or states. Bringing the Iksan headquarters or the Bishops' Conference building in as Seoul states

## Appendix — The Five Axes and Their Numbers

The axes that changed rules in the story were five. They were read only behind the centuries, successions, and daily life of the body. Remove the axes and what remained was a dynasty game with vassal oaths alone. Those rules were written by [Ambitions](../characters/Ambitions-and-Relations.md).

| Axis | Slot | What moves | On failure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Divinization of wreckage | Wreckage-rite right `Salv` | Retrieval and scheduled pilgrimage recorded as piety | Read retrieval as plunder and chapel authority is shaved |
| Deification of old-world symbols | Symbol pedestal `Sym` | Complex plaques, the subway mark, the Arisu mark rise to divine seats | Take down the pedestal and that station's creation story goes blank |
| Consumption rites | Distribution-consumption faith | Old currency, distribution paths, ad frames become rite | If ledger or ad is false, that day's justice is void |
| Machine worship | Machine piety `Mech` | Touching and mending rolling stock, substations, lift machinery is rite | When restart fails, the maintenance house rises into heresy dispute |
| Sect/culture split matrix | Lineage × culture cells | Public, minority, hidden, heresy differ per cell | Lock one cell as all Seoul's state faith and hidden cells multiply |

`Salv` `Sym` `Mech` ran 0–100. The defaults were design assumptions. At the opening, a station with a local chapel stood at 40, without one at 15. The three strata (lineage → faith → doctrine) were the vessel holding these five axes.

Recorded as plunder, `Salv` fell and reputation was shaved. `Sym` rose when the pedestal was kept public and fell on demolition. A station that demolished lost the protagonist of its creation story, so other lineages tried to plant their own symbols. The acts that raised `Mech` were successful repair, restart, and the offering of parts to the chapel.

The ground cell k of the legitimacy formula followed the diplomacy document. Faith locked or opened that cell.
