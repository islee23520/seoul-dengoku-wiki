# The Stories

The name the generation of 2126 memorized as the first line of the creation story remained the Great Blackout. The name the canon set was [motive-power revocation](../overview/World-Unbinding.md), and the power cut of that night stood so close to a symptom left in the creation story that it was never read as the event's own name.

No year of the common era was attached to the oral titles. The years of the real events from the revocation of 2026 to the opening of 2126 belonged to [the Hundred-Year Annals of Seoul Dengoku](../chronology/Century-Annals.md), and the creation oral titles locked by the [Scenario Timeline](../chronology/Scenario-Timeline.md) were the First Water Compact, the military-household register dispute, the Sixteen Banners, the Merit Subjects' Winter, and the Second Water Compact. The night of the Great Blackout remained the front seat of that list in the oral record.

This page called for no new plotlines. It only gathered the seats already written and the testimony, and the prohibition was the same as the timeline's: a name entered as a founder in the ledgers and a person seated at the opening were never to be read as one body. The way rice and water repeated that oral record at the windows was carried by [Food Culture](Food-Culture.md), and the way the pulpit and the altar read the same night as a blessing by [the Faith-and-Culture Schism](Faith-Culture-Schism.md).

## The Night of the Great Blackout

The oral record called it the Great Blackout. The canon called it motive-power revocation, and when the fleet calls, GNSS, the mobile networks, and the licensing servers stopped waiting for one another, the grid SCADA belonged to the same mesh and the lights folded.

The Yeouido treatment plant and the Ttukseom workshops saved their own jurisdictions first. The emergency officers of the Amsa guard and the records wire exercised emergency powers on the same night; once the joint command mesh broke, living-sphere emergency committees arose in every sphere, and when those powers were returned stayed the seed of a century of legitimacy disputes.

The sound of seals at the threshold of the Seoul Station stacks traced that hour back. An oral account that the treatment, rail, and records officers each seized emergency powers at the stacks' threshold remained with the Republic of Korea Government, and at the Magok emergency committee a scribe who had written the times of the powers' exercise and return to the minute left the diary of the creation.

On the Sinnae platform a draft advertisement remained. It was the memory that on the night the joint command mesh broke, copy claiming the developmental legitimacy of the great powers entered the loudspeaker script chest; no witness of the 2126 generation lived that night, so the diary, the sound of the seals, and the draft advertisement were read only as copies.

Talk that set nuclear war, climate collapse, or plague as the first cause could remain a week's rumor. What the creation oral record raised to its first line was the night the lights folded.

## The First Water Compact

The surviving treatment plants and the pump parts lay scattered. When the Yeouido treatment officer and the Ttukseom pump overseer struck a provisional compact trading water for parts, the hands that read it as public rights and the hands that read it as a protection contract divided on the spot, and the seed was planted from which the Water Compact, the Sermon Registry State, and the Escort Protection State grew into water great powers.

The scales of the Noryangjin berth testified to that afternoon. The scene of water and parts loading onto different spheres remained in the oral record as market testimony, and the anecdote that the last witness of the Yeongdeungpo-side exchange list heard a fire alarm ring down the vault corridor just before the original was sealed hung on the same title.

No new dialogue was made. The berth and the vault corridor were already written as the testimony seats of that compact, and the window habit that water came first and the pot followed grew out of this oral record. The saying that water without the Arisu mark was guest water, unfit for rites, was locked by [the Faith-and-Culture Schism](Faith-Culture-Schism.md), and the consequence — that the ration pot waited for that mark — was carried by [Food Culture](Food-Culture.md).

## The Second Water Compact

The provisional pumps and train parts ran out their lives. The officers of Yeouido, Ttukseom, Amsa, and Jegi-dong at that time wrote the minimum line, the standards, the emergency convoys, and the termination conditions again, the Ttukseom overseer stood surety for central water and parts, and that wording remained in the ledgers up to the third contract of the time. The minimum water right was the lesser states' support, and the protection clause was read as the grounds for the Amsa elders' intervention.

Testimony that boxes were opened at the Yongsan transshipment shed lay layered. It was the seat where the spent pumps and train parts were checked by hand; at the Changdong public scales the spent wheels and brake boxes were checked together with the standards seal, and an oral account also held of escorting a sealed chest inscribed with the minimum-line clause back and forth between Ttukseom and Sinjeong.

On the Jeongdong General Labor Union side a witness of the signature remained. The hand that would decide the Second Water Compact's force still kept the hidden seals of the original audit sheet under seal, and the choice of reviving the seals with heat or washing them away with water stayed as the story's branch, never carried into a new plotline of the creation.

The third water contract of opening day opened together with its expiry. The central water-and-parts contract handed down from the creation oral record ended at the same moment, and the cell the oral record left blank by writing no termination condition shook the price of water and the medical convoys a century later.

## Other Titles of the Same Creation

The titles the timeline locked without year counts did not end at three. No new oral record was added; the titles already in existence were set side by side.

The military-household register dispute was an oral record split over household-unit service registration. Before the outer raids and the shortage of restoration hands, the Amsa military faction demanded it and the Bukhansan Bogukmun refugee delegates refused; where voluntary service and citizenship bound together, the league of the Jegi-dong and Bogukmun elders remained, and where forced registration remained, the Amsa officer network grew. The register citizens and the escort protection of that time were copies of this dispute.

The Sixteen Banners was the declaration that the central emergency committee dissolved. The sixteen spheres raised their own charters and offices and became five great powers and eleven lesser states; the states that received accreditation gained legitimacy but owed the Records Bureau. Testimony remained at the seat of that declaration that the accredited Gate charter of Seoul Station and the unaccredited passage customs were folded separately into a chest, and the disposition of [the Sixteen States of Seoul](../factions/Sixteen-States.md) at the opening of 2126 was read as the descendant of those banners, fixed by no year's photograph.

The Merit Subjects' Winter was an oral record of demands made all at once. When the founding merit subjects held out offices, ration rights, and hereditary rights together, shaving the rewards grew coups and lavishing them scattered the titles, so some states became condominiums and some became court-guard states. The memory that only the relief boxes stayed empty clung to that winter's transcription in the oral record.

In the empty century no title was written anew. The living-sphere banners and the water ledgers ran on, but the faces of the founding merit subjects and the everyday life of 2026 became oral record, copies, and myth, and the reason the stations, the sluices, and the depots formed the skeleton that held those copies was written by [the Structures Compendium](../structures/Structures.md).
