# Values and Policy Scales

What moved a person was not only temperament, ambition, and fear. Faced with the same issue, the means chosen diverged according to **ten values cells**. Organizations likewise took no eternal national personality; they stood on **the values of the current charter** and **policy slots**. When a leader was replaced, the numbers engraved in the charter went through a natural re-review, in the same vein as the "no fixed national personality" principle mentioned in [Ambitions](../characters/Ambitions-and-Relations.md).

The scale ran from −100 to 100. Zero meant only that the axis had not yet tipped; it did not stand for the virtue of indifference. When registering a new person or organization, these cells were never left empty. Filling the age indicators of the 422 from this table, however, was to be avoided. The axes of sexual desire and taste that also moved persons were reflected, while sexual narratives involving minors were strictly excluded. The numeric ledgers followed [values-cast.json](../name-pools/values-cast.json) and [values-orgs.json](../name-pools/values-orgs.json).

As comparison groups, the ethics pairs of Stellaris (authoritarian·egalitarian, xenophobe·xenophile, militarist·pacifist, materialist·spiritualist) and its policy slots, and the central, innovative, and diplomatic slots of Europa Universalis IV, were consulted. But the galaxy-species names of the originals, Christian ideology-group names, and distinctive event lines were not carried over.

## Person Desires and Tastes

If values were the gradient on public issues, desire worked as the axis on which **with whom and how one wanted to entangle** shook decisions. It was read together with ambition and fear. Bonds made without the counterpart's consent, abuse of office, and material involving minors did not go into these cells.

| # | Axis | −100 | +100 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Longing | Low · restraint | High |
| 2 | Possessiveness | Sharing · open seats | Monopoly · jealousy |
| 3 | Risk | Safety · predictability | Danger · the forbidden |
| 4 | Display | Hiding · the private | Display · the public |
| 5 | Duration | The moment | Long companionship |

Orientation: opposite sex / same sex / both sexes / none / fluid. Bonding: monogamy / multi-partner / contract cohabitation / non-exclusive / none.

```text
Desires: longing _ | possessiveness _ | risk _ | display _ | duration _ | orientation _ | bonding _
```

## Organization Values

Organization values were likewise built on the **same ten axes** as persons. The numbers were scored against the current charter, council majorities, and ledger practice. The authority number Han Jaemok carried as an individual could differ from the authority number of the Water Compact he belonged to, and it was precisely in that difference that issues budded.

## The Eight Organization Policy Cells

As in the Stellaris policy system, **each slot was given choices.** The numbers here were not slot strength but simple choice codes.

| Migrants | Expulsion / quarantine admission / citizenship | Gate registers |

| Records | Closed / cross-verified / fully public | Wills and ledgers |
| Technology | House secret / licensed sharing / joint ledger | Pump and cooling keys |
| Trade | Blockade / licensed / open market | Auctions and convoys |
| Succession | Bloodline / adoptive apprenticeship / open examination | Stewards and elders |
| Bonding | Monogamy license / contract cohabitation / house bonding / military-register bonding / open bonding | Marriage and cohabitation ledgers. Organizations did not assign sexual taste |

## The 1001-Person Generation Path

The canon of named persons was limited to the **1001** belonging to the sixteen states. The corridor seeds and the 100 not yet through review (the random additional roster) were not counted into that 1001. Each added person required the cast card contract, the ten cells above, and the desire cells filled without gaps. Generation was not complete by merely spinning a random seed and mechanically copying fear lines.

The order of generation was as follows.

1. A suitable name was chosen from the name pool, and carefully checked against how the person referred to themselves.
2. Origin, languages, trade, affiliation, position, conscription record, armament state, and generation were written into their cells. Ages were not filled in without clear grounds.
3. The ten value numbers, the five desire cells, and orientation and bonding information were entered. Whether these settings pointed naturally in the same direction as ambition and fear was reviewed. Sexual narratives involving minors were strictly excluded.
4. Values were adjusted so that **at least one axis** clashed with the charter values and policies of the affiliated organization. When every number overlapped the organization perfectly, no issue proper to the person arose.
5. Relations with other persons and the events that triggered conflict were recorded concretely.
6. Only after all of this was a person registered as a pre-approval candidate. As stated, the corridor seeds and the roster-100 candidates were not hastily put into the sixteen-state 1001.

When newly writing or revising organization settings (sixteen states, HC, HP, XT, conscription remnants), the ten value cells and the eight policy cells had to be revised together in the same commit.
