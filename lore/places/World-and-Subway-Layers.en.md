# Seoul and the Subway Layers

The stairs that led down from the concourse to the platform, and the corner where a transfer corridor bent, lay over each other in a single picture.

## The Vertical Structure of the World

Read Seoul only as an administrative map painted flat in districts like Jongno-gu, Jung-gu, and Yeongdeungpo-gu, and the ground on which the states of the reconstruction stood never came into view. The places where people actually ate, slept, and passed goods were floors of different heights — the Seoul Station concourse, the Sindorim transfer, the Wangsimni tracks, the deep levels toward Amsa — and each floor carried its own work and its own dangers. The world was the graph that joined those floors.

| Layer | Primary function | Representative risk |
|---|---|---|
| Surface ruins | Resource gathering, solar power, long-range observation | Weather, collapse, exposure, clashes between powers |
| Station concourse | Markets, habitation, administration, information | Crowding, fire, power struggles |
| Platform · tracks | Movement, defensive lines, freight | Bottlenecks, ambushes, power cuts |
| Transfer corridors | Political and logistics links between lines | Blockades, transit tolls, congestion |
| Deep tunnels | Covert movement, maintenance facilities | Flooding, darkness, communications loss |
| Depot-adjacent strongholds | Repair, parts, bulk storage | Strategic sieges, fire |

## Graph Construction

The world-truth graph carried the stations and the sectors and the links between them all at once. The screen raised only the lines needed at the moment and the detail of the chosen station. Choosing Seoul Station unfolded that station's concourse and platforms, and the tunnel running from Yeongdeungpo to Sindorim could be lit on its own.

## Dynamic Links

A link was never erased; only its state changed. Every pass from normal to hazardous, controlled, or blockaded, and every turn to flooding, collapse, or temporary repair, recorded its cause alongside. So the same view told why a road had broken and what had to be mended before it opened again.

The effect of these links on actual supply throughput and on isolation and recovery was computed by [Logistics and Infrastructure](../economy/Logistics-and-Infrastructure.md), and the control and service of the linked stations were owned by [Strongholds and Territory](../economy/Strongholds-and-Territory.md).
