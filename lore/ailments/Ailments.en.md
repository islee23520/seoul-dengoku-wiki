# Ailments and Symptoms

Disease was not a new plague fallen from the sky. Even in 2026, Seoul's tap water had flowed through treatment centers and pipes. Hospitals and clinics had counted stock by the essential-medicines list, and asbestos, mold, and machine coolant had already been in the subways, the schools, and the apartments. On the night of the [motive-power revocation](../overview/World-Unbinding.md), when remote control and grid power went, those three layers remained in human hands alone.

A century later, when a cough went round the concourse, the medical officer opened the tanks, the medicine chests, and the damp walls before looking for a curse of creation. Nuclear war, climate collapse, and plague were not left as first causes in [the Scenario Timeline](../chronology/Scenario-Timeline.md). The turbid water and counterfeit medicine of summer spread first. When water shook, medical convoys ran late, and when medicine shook, the ration windows' trust shook with it.

## Waterborne Disease

Seoul's drinking water had been a system that treated raw Han water and sent it through pipes. The Yeongdeungpo Arisu treatment center pushed water toward Gangseo, Guro, Geumcheon, and Yangcheon. Ttukseom took Seongdong, Jung-gu, and Yongsan; Guui took Gwangjin, Jungnang, and Dongdaemun; Amsa took Gangnam and Gangdong and the southern districts. The Gangbuk treatment center sat in Namyangju and Gwangam in Hanam, outside Seoul, not easy for [the Sixteen States of Seoul](../factions/Sixteen-States.md) to control.

After the revocation, when the grid SCADA died, the pumps, the chlorine injection, and the turbidity tests came down to human watches. The stations with tanks held out. Remote safety control was dead. When the Yeouido sluices and the Ttukseom pumps could not share parts, the water in the pipes clouded first, and the standing water of flooded deep tunnels and transfer corridors mixed into that cloud.

The waterborne symptoms were the result of that mixing. Diarrhea, fever, and dehydration stopped the ration lines. Labor endurance broke together with the water shortage.

Eating the tank fish and drawing drinking water used the same surface. When the waste of the cells raising carp, crucian carp, and catfish mixed with the drinking-water ledger, that day's turbid water became the evening soup. [Food Culture](../culture/Food-Culture.md), which shared the tank water, and the [structures](../structures/Structures.md) of sluices and treatment plants tangled with each other.

In the first summer, when storms passed, some water tests failed and counterfeit medicine began to circulate. The research council demanded disclosure; the Water Director-General demanded delay. That conflict bore directly on the Noryangjin frozen stock. When turbid water went round, the medicine ration was cut with it.

The [Flood-Mold Breath Cluster](../bestiary/groups/Hostile-Group-G10.md) grew out of the repeated flooding of the Bukhansan Bogukmun corridor and the lower logistics warehouses of Garak and Jamsil. That name described an environmental ecology and never meant a social class. Wet concrete, the heat of grain, and delayed ventilation raised the coughs. A cough was not a moral defect.

## Side Effects of Stockpiled Medicine

The Korea of 2026 had managed state stock by the essential-medicines list. Antibiotics, antipyretics, and fluids had been divided among hospitals, clinics, and military medical stores, and preparations needing refrigerated cells like insulin had stood on the same list. The Jegi-dong medicinal-herb market had turned herbal medicines and general pharmaceuticals together.

Only that year's packaging remained. The expiry dates had crossed a century. The Medical Neutrality League's underground sorting block woke before the people at midnight, repeating its routine of pushing unlabeled boxes into the wrong cryo cells, and the Standards Alliance side's parts racks and rollers clung to that line.

The medical officer read the sealing tape. Tablets that had changed color and syrups whose layers had separated went to the disposal cells. Still, at stations where the ration ran late, hands remained that swallowed the 2026 stockpile medicine as it was.

The side effects made no new pathogen. Taking efficacy-dead antibiotics a few days longer swelled the diarrhea and left the fever undropped. An antipyretic past its date only churned the stomach, and insulin cut from refrigeration deceived the body even when the numbers looked right. When counterfeit boxes filled that blank, the Jegi-dong treatment guild and Noryangjin's frozen credit were tested at once.

The medicine shortage stopped treatment acts and laid a great burden on [Economy and Production](../economy/Economy-and-Production.md) and the distribution network. The draft medical charter that medicine chests must not be taken as spoils remained, but so did the fear that if one great power monopolized the purchase of antibiotics, the guild's legitimacy would fall in a single season. Swallowing the remaining packaging took over the stations' medical role.

## Hazardous Environments After the Blackout

Asbestos had been used as insulation and fireproofing in subway concourses and platforms, in school special-purpose rooms, and in the ceilings of 1970s and 1980s apartments. While grid power ran, sealing paint and ventilation had held down the dust. After the night of the revocation, when the pumps stopped, flooding soaked the ceiling materials. Once they dried and people passed through, the fibers rose.

That was why the breathing protection and lamps an explorer used to open a deep tunnel were the starting point of the trade zone. Mold bloomed on the same walls as asbestos. When quarters behind on the ventilation roster, the heat of ration grain, and condensation-beaded drinking pipes met in one lattice, the coughs and eye sting came first.

After the Yeouido Investment Union's frozen stacks stopped, unable to bear the transmitters' waste heat, condensation ran down the low-temperature inspection window that had led to the Gate Military Government. Calling the mold panic the curse of one sect settled only after the order of cooler use and the responsibility for ventilation were divided.

Coolant went onto no drinking ledger. When the liquids left in depot heat exchangers, substation cooling loops, and medical refrigerators stagnated, a film grew on the metal surfaces. The sensors put out steady numbers and the far branch pipes clogged. The field called that film the Coolant-Index Fungal Body.

The warning to separate the drinking and coolant ledgers held also before the [G19](../bestiary/groups/Hostile-Group-G19.md) swarms and the equipment of [the old technology](../technology/Lost-Technology-Lineage.md). When a watchman poured coolant into a tank, waterborne disease spread.

The first floors south of the Han and the tributary banks were monsoon storage or abandoned space. The dwelling zones were pushed above the third floor, and the ration windows fell back to the [buildings inside the embankment](../places/Building-Reuse-Geography.md). A person who slept in a wet first floor, used a special room of rising asbestos dust as quarters, and mistook a coolant puddle for a washbasin became the medical officer's patient.

[Machines](../people-and-machines/People-and-Machines.md) and people shared the same air. Stepping into a [station interior](../places/Station-Interior-Construction.md) without guaranteed ventilation and water supply exposed a person to contaminated air and water.
