## Common Forms and Variants

The Retired-Line Maintenance-Train Corps moved between the southwestern exit lines and the Yongsan retired and turnback lines, repeating unfinished maintenance orders with a defect-map controller and function-divided consists. It was no combat train and could not find new routes beyond the sealed main lines by itself. It could grind temporary crossing plates down as cracks, or pass a real crack by classifying it as a joint.

An unsealed defect map and exit-line power were the base ecology, and from the defect-map locomotive to the twin-torch head car the units were divided like gauge wheels, sleeper flats, insulated joints, and point wedges. No form revived an animal; it only coupled abandoned maintenance cars into existing consists and produced no new car bodies. Later cars absent from this deployment also remained subtypes of the same consists and were not invented here.

## Units and Battle Formation

The F02 Switchgear Panel split and recorded the two waveforms but could not fix defect coordinates; only the HP04 operating supervisor's halt writ approved exits and stops by kilometer-post. The senses were the track gauge wheels, ultrasonic inspection, axle heat, power-feed arc sound, and point contacts. Cars that hit the fuel floor or axle overheat fell out of the consist and kept only gauging on a designated siding.

The player gave units movement, guard, engagement, withdrawal, and specialist work orders. The counters below were the acts of units executing those orders, not a skill list for directly controlling heroes. The human soldier squad's cap of twenty and the separate hero rules were not carried over into counts of animals or machines.

## Habitat Record

At the end of the southwestern exit line, a maintenance car's grinding stone bit into an already smooth joint again, spitting sparks.

The onboard port's defect map, pushed by XT03 transshipment vibration, mixed real cracks with old coordinates, and the welding and sleeper cars behind took the same kilometer-post as work targets.

The Retired-Line Maintenance-Train Corps was no combat train but an ecology of a defect-map controller and function-divided consists repeating unfinished maintenance orders.

It moved between the Yeongdeungpo-line depot exit and the Wangsimni coalition's Yongsan retired and turnback lines, but it could not find new routes beyond the sealed main lines by itself.

Track gauge wheels, ultrasonic inspection, axle heat, power-feed arc sound, and point contacts were its senses.

It could grind a temporary crossing plate away as a crack, or pass a real crack by classifying it as a joint.

When transshipment vibration and welding arcs overlapped, it fell into a human-confirmation wait before cutting.

The F02 Switchgear Panel split and recorded the two waveforms but could not fix defect coordinates.

Only the HP04 operating supervisor, holding the halt writ stamped with the original hash, approved exits and stops by kilometer-post.

Depot power feed, onboard batteries, welding-generator fuel cells, and regenerative braking were the energy sources.

Welding wire, grinding stones, sleeper anchors, insulated joints, lubricant, and coolant were consumed.

Cars that hit the fuel floor or axle overheat fell out of the consist and kept only gauging on a designated siding.

It produced no new car bodies and only coupled abandoned maintenance cars into existing consists.

Cutting the power feed whole stopped the wrongful severance but took away the night crack gauging and the emergency welding too.

The next maintenance window opened only when the supervisor recovered the previous halt writ and inserted a new exclusion list.

Emergence came when the unsealed defect map meshed with exit power; spread came when neighboring consists received the same coordinate list.

Wire exhaustion and overheating made stagnation, and when maintenance windows and sealed sections were disclosed, negotiation became possible.

Dissolution was not burning the depot but recovering the wrong map, locking the points, and separating the consists onto sidings.

The gauge wheels and manual towing had to be left standing, preserving the ability to tend real cracks.

### G18-SC3 · The Return Without Flame
