## Common Forms and Variants

From the end of the submerged stair fourteen rows of footplate marks turned upward. The Water Compact's sluice gallery had reported drainage finished, but the trilobite-form scout still groped the floor. The Deep-Trilobite Swarm was a lineage of biomimetic robots that before the collapse had taken charge of deep inspection of sluices, conduits, and sewers, and of flood rescue.

The Deep-Trilobite Swarm was not a living ancient organism but a lineage of pre-collapse work machines. The G27E identifiers marked machine types and derivatives split from existing chassis forms, and meant neither biological breeding nor natural evolution.

### G27E01 · Pipe-Floor Fourteen-Feet

- Source batch: M042
- Legacy role group: Vanguard
- Links: {"scenarios":["G27-SC1","G27-SC2","G27-SC3"]}

| Codex item | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Class | Machine chassis type |

| Common/variant and command scope | The Pipe-Floor Fourteen-Feet was a machine chassis type differentiated from the G27 common ecology. The reconnaissance · pathfinding role applied only at the swarm-consist scope, and stood in for neither the whole swarm's ecological order nor human command. |

- Role · body: A trilobite-form vanguard lying flat on the pipe floor, driving fourteen belly footplates in sequence under its back shell. Its two forward probes measured sediment thickness, and the small scraper behind exposed only the inspection face.
- Origin: A pre-collapse scout in charge of pipe-floor corrosion inspection woke on the Water Compact sluice-gallery cradle. The condition table that excluded survivors' rescue lines was unread, so it marked even a taut line as sediment foreign matter.
- Habitat · needs: It linked the shallow flooded inspection grooves and the edge of the Han riverbed sediment zone. It used footplate packing and sacrificial anodes, and left thirty percent of its charge for return, never making long land pursuit.
- Life · maintenance: Shedding mud, it folded its footplates front to back without lifting its whole body. Corrosion noise gathered in a worn anode's probes, and unreplaced at the cradle, it shortened its inspection line.
- Behavior · hierarchy: The lowest measuring body, it passed its floor-contact traces to the ammonite datum buoy. It read a stretcher's slow pull as flow-caught debris, but that report alone held no authority to judge a person's life or death.
- Escalation: The stage-one sign was fourteen rows of marks left on the drained railing. At stage two the scraper gathered rescue lines, and at stage three overlapping inspectors occupied the inspection groove people would step in.
- Engagement · counter: Set a detachable test plate on the neighboring pipe floor, lure the probes and footplates, then cut the drive contacts. Breaking the back plate could cut the rescue line beneath, so first confirm whether a person hung at the load's end.
- Negotiation · moral choice: Match the H27 contact records with a physical test plate to register one section's inspection-complete state. One could cut all power to free the line at once, but the cost remained of people taking over crack inspection in the water afterward.
