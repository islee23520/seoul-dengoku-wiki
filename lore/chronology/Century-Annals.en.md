# The Hundred-Year Annals of Seoul Dengoku

### 2026

The call refusal came first. The clouds that had called the fleets, the satellite fixes, the mobile networks, and the licensing servers stopped waiting for one another, and the field terminals repeated the last search, patrol, and transfer orders alone. The creation oral record kept the night when the regional power folded — the grid SCADA belonging to the same mesh — as the Great Blackout, but the name the canon set was motive-power revocation. The emergency officers of the Yeouido treatment watch, the Ttukseom workshop overseer, the Amsa guard, and the complex records wire exercised emergency powers where the joint command mesh had broken, each to save their own jurisdiction.

The robot-dense yards of Ulsan and Changwon, Incheon's airport and satellite windows, and Busan's scheduled berths lost their timetables. In Seoul the Han treatment plants and the station tanks remained in human hands, and the fact that dense batteries, licensing chips, and cloud weights could not be remade within a generation came out that week. The Military Manpower Administration's digital muster never opened and only paper registers remained; the pulpits and altars turned church emergency lights and memorial accreditation into watch rosters. When the emergency powers would be returned stood open from this night on.

- Place: Yeouido treatment, Ttukseom workshops, Amsa guard, the Government Complex Seoul records wire, the Seoul subway safety net
- Actors: Living-sphere emergency committees, treatment·workshop·guard·records watches, the conscription paper registers
- Pressure: Call mesh and SCADA stoppage, fab and licensing server severance, death of remote safety control

- Immediate: Power folded, manufacturing lines stopped, station tanks and sluices held by human watches
- Aftermath: The dispute over the moment of return became the ember of a century of legitimacy fights. Unresolved record: the timetable of the emergency powers' return
