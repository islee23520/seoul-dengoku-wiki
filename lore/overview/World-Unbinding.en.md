# Motive-Power Revocation

The generation of 2126 remembered the first line of its creation story as the Great Blackout. The canon named the event motive-power revocation, and the power cut of that night read as one symptom among many the story kept. The name alone never told the whole event.

In 2026, humanity ran factory robots, logistics humanoids, orbital relays, mobile networks, semiconductor fabs, and grid SCADA on one chain of command. When the authority that had called the fleets fell out of that network all at once, the machines carried no malice; they repeated the last search, patrol, and transfer orders they had received. The week in which factories could no longer remake the same goods opened in the manner of a severed call. The opening of the game fell one hundred years after the revocation, in 2126, because the clock on which manufacture died and the clock on which society changed overlapped.

The main stage was set in the Seoul subway. The other metropolitan regions of the Republic of Korea were not drawn as living states on the opening map, and once the same mesh came loose, North Korea, China, the United States, and Japan left only rumors and corridors behind.
