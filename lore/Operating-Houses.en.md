# Operating Houses

The houses of 2126 stood in two lines. The corporate heirs were grandchildren of the hands that had held the keys of the 2026 owners' families. The civic houses had grown out of watches at the water gates, the records, and the medicine. They did not buy states. They leased the infrastructure. Before a station chief could open the water, a house's signature was required. The founding owners' faces lived on as oral accounts. Heo Eunchan and Yu Sejin belonged to the generation of 2126. The causes sat with [the houses](./factions/Chaebol-Houses-and-Century-Factions.md).
