### Person Jeong Yura

**Life.** The generation of 2126. Contract audit at the site of the Kyunghyang Shinmun building, 3 Jeongdong-gil, Jung-gu. The taboo of the Record Blade was forgery. The union roster of the Jeongdong office became an emergency muster, and when the factories died the right to strike turned into a right of passage. Jeong Yura became chair after keeping the protection-standard drawings of the Suseo Construction House in an unbroken line. The Suseo base remained masterless unclaimed land, and the federation office at City Hall Station acted the part of the state. In the season when three great powers forced three different water contracts on the lesser states at once, Jeong Yura tried to put exit clauses and audit rights on one sheet. That sheet became the frame of the Lesser States Council. Jang Sehwa's convoy carried the sheet.
**Office.** Rank: Chair. Title: Chair of the Jeongdong General Labor Union. Livelihood byname: Contract Auditor.
**Martial Path.** Record Blade.
**Anecdote.** When the three contracts arrived at once, Jeong Yura stacked the three sheets, left only the exit clauses, and sealed the rest. The seal fell in the same week as Yun Seorin's suspended ratification.
**House.** Suseo Construction House (HC13). Steward. The protection-standard drawings were this house's key.
**Relations.** Technology sharing with Jeong Hojun. A ratification rivalry with Yun Seorin. The Lesser States Council with Jang Sehwa.
