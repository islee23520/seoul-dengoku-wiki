# Characters

The state did not raise its hand. A person of rank proposed, and others stamped or refused it. The grammar followed the character entries of namuwiki and the officer entries of Romance of the Three Kingdoms. Offices belong to [Offices and Ranks](../offices/Offices-and-Ranks.md). Martial paths belong to [Martial Paths](../culture/Martial-Paths.md). Houses share one ledger with [Chaebol Houses and Century Factions](../factions/Chaebol-Houses-and-Century-Factions.md) and the steward registry of [Operating Houses](../Operating-Houses.md).

Names were written as one surname syllable plus one or two given-name syllables. A self-proclaimed king was not obliged to carry a single-syllable given name. Lines, families, and battles of real people were not copied.

## Han Jaemok

Stationmaster of the Water Compact. The man who sought to bind the locks of the western Han River under his own charter. As long as water came out, the place stayed alive.

**Life.** The generation of 2126. He grew up in the hands of the duty line of the Yeongdeungpo Arisu Water Purification Center under Seoul Arisu headquarters. He knew the first Water Compact of the creation story only by oral record. What his grandfather left behind was not a company name but the lock key and the duty roster. From that key Han Jaemok learned the habit of never opening the water gates before the ration-district representatives signed their joint seal. The stationmaster's seat opened not by election but after the hand that held the key kept an unbroken line. The Yeouido financial halls split off into a neighboring state, and the seating of I Seodam as district head of Yangcheon was the trade that left that joint signature in the institutions.

**Office.** Rank: Stationmaster. Title: Stationmaster of the Water Compact. Livelihood byname: Water Director-General.

**Martial Path.** Sluice Hand. His master's line ran through the purification duty line of the creation oral record. The taboo was concealment of turbid water. Whoever hid turbid water had his transmission cut off.

**Anecdote.** One season before Im Hajun disappeared, the turbidity of the western pipelines exceeded the standard. Han Jaemok was pressed to keep the supply flowing and could not open the valve of Park Nuri, the purification supervisor. That day's signature remained suspended, and I Seodam took the suspension as the grounds for Yangcheon's veto.

**House.** Yeongdeungpo Sluice House (HP01). Steward. Opening the water gates required this house's signature.

**Relations.** A parts alliance with Gang Minseo. A dispute with O Haerin over price disclosure. A contract-ratification dispute with Yun Seorin. A dual executive with I Seodam, district head of Yangcheon.

## Gang Minseo

Chair of the Standards Alliance. The person who sought to set the standards of the repairing hand into citizenship and one ledger.

**Life.** The generation of 2126. She grew up in the Guro and Geumcheon industrial complex guilds. The elders of the Wheel Hammer expelled any conversion without a standard. In the winter when the Escort Protection State demanded restoration-service registration as the price of parts, Gang Minseo put the hands that refused registration onto the citizenship line. That line became the grounds of her chair's seat. If the guards subdued the workshops, the Standards Alliance became a munitions factory. The Daerim dormitories were an enclave within this state. That was why Gang Minseo backed Baek On's roster.

**Office.** Rank: Chair. Title: Chair of the Standards Alliance. Livelihood byname: Fabrication Council Mediator.

**Martial Path.** Wheel Hammer. She expelled any conversion without a standard.

**Anecdote.** When the Escort Protection State held out a roster of restoration-service workers instead of a box of pump parts, Gang Minseo sealed the box and sent it back. That seal remained as a debt between Han Jaemok's western water supply and Bae Woojin's military administration.

**House.** Guro Alloy House (HC07). Steward. The standards drawing cabinet was this house's key.

**Relations.** Staked pump parts for Han Jaemok. Disputed the scope of technical disclosure with Jeong Hojun. Backed Baek On's refugee citizenship.

## Jeong Hojun

President of Yangjae Machineworks Company. The person who sought to build a registry that held no researcher hostage.

**Life.** The generation of 2126. His hands came from the field-test line at the Yangjae headquarters site, 12 Heonreung-ro, Seocho-gu. In 2026 that yard already ran humanoids, and when cloud learning died in the week of the revocation, a maintenance house kept the company name. The taboo of the Record Blade was forgery. When a motion rose in the board to bind researchers into a hereditary vocation in the name of protection, Jeong Hojun moved the specimens into the common-property column and struck out the hostage clause. That deletion became the condition of his investiture as president. It read as the same habit as Jeong Yura's exit clauses. If Gang Minseo locked the standards, the Yangjae registry could not follow. The assay key of the Magok Life House stayed in the Magok unclaimed zone, and they fought over the scope of disclosure.

**Office.** Rank: President. Title: President of Yangjae Machineworks Company. Livelihood byname: Chief Researcher for Life Safety.

**Martial Path.** Record Blade. Whoever forged lost his hand.

**Anecdote.** When a specimen of counterfeit medicine entered the Yangjae archive, Jeong Hojun broke the seal before the city hall side could request an audit. The opened seal met Ryu Eunbi's sickbed ledger.

**House.** Magok Life House (HC03). Steward. The assay registry was this house's key.

**Relations.** A disclosure contract with Jeong Yura. A disclosure-scope dispute with Gang Minseo.

## Im Hajun

Moderator of the Sermon Registry State State. Missing just before 2126. The man who left the guarantee clause of the assembly's roster in the ledger.

**Life.** The generation of 2126. A descendant of the guarantor line of the second Water Compact in the creation oral record, though the body did not overlap with it. The roster of the assembly hall of the Sermon Registry at 29 Daehak-ro 3-gil, Yeonji-dong, Jongno-gu, became the duty roster of the night of the Great Blackout, and a century later the pulpit real estate that survived sat on the Gangnam side, so Samseong Station held the capital. The hand that learned pump standards on the Ttukseom heat-source duty line passed only half of the hammer to his adopted child Im Chowon. Before his disappearance he left three testaments. Before the testaments could be ratified, Han Jaemok's western proposal moved. The steward's seat was received provisionally by Park Nuri. The Moderator's seat stood empty.

**Office.** Rank: Moderator. Title: Moderator of the Sermon Registry State State. Livelihood byname: Superintendent of Pump Craft. The seat stood empty.

**Martial Path.** Wheel Hammer. The transmission passed only half to his adopted child Im Chowon.

**Anecdote.** On the night before his disappearance, Im Hajun opened only the original box of the pump-washing process and sealed the copies. The key of the sealed box was received together with the three testaments.

**House.** The line of former heads of the Ttukseom Heat-Source House (HC04). The steward was Park Nuri. After the disappearance the key was divided.

**Relations.** Adoptive father of Im Chowon. One point of the water triangle of Han Jaemok, Bae Woojin, and Gang Minseo.

## Bae Woojin

Commander of the Escort Protection State. The person who sought to raise the shield that guarded the water into the law of a military administration.

**Life.** The generation of 2126. His hands came from the conscription remnants. The taboo of the Escort Shield was hiding a civil roster behind the shield. It was not Bae Woojin's ancestors that bound the eastern purification works and the Godeok depot under military supervision, but the fact that the Amsa Arisu Water Purification Center remained inside Seoul. The commander's seat opened after the chief of staff exercised emergency supervision. He held no house key. The barrier gate of the Changdong Protection House belonged to I Hongwon. That Bae Woojin demanded restoration-service registration as the price of parts was the hand of the military administration, and no house signature followed it.

**Office.** Rank: Commander. Title: Commander of the Escort Protection State. Livelihood byname: Water-Supply Escort Chief.

**Martial Path.** Escort Shield. Few could attach to it at once. Hiding a civil roster behind the shield meant expulsion.

**Anecdote.** After Gang Minseo sealed the parts box and returned it, Bae Woojin moved the registration roster into the military administration's box. The roster never became citizenship and remained only as the escort's debt.

**House.** The escort was the hand of the military administration and no house key followed it. The steward of the Changdong Protection House (HC08) was I Hongwon.

**Relations.** Water-gate tactics with Han Jaemok. Received the eastern military rations from Nam Yungyeong. Stood against Gang Minseo over service registration.

## Im Chowon

Teacher. No land. The adopted child of the missing Moderator.

**Life.** The generation of 2126. She grew up only beside pumps. That Im Hajun passed her half the hammer was not succession to the Moderator's seat but transmission of craft. Her rank was Teacher. If she testified to the standards before the testaments opened, her adoptive father's seat moved. If she did not testify, Park Nuri's provisional stewardship hardened. The reason she stood between the Yangjae maintenance yard and the Samseong Station roster was that half-transmission.

**Office.** Rank: Teacher. No title. Livelihood: pump technician.

**Martial Path.** Wheel Hammer. Her master was Im Hajun. Only half the transmission reached her.

**Anecdote.** On the shift after the disappearance, Im Chowon did not open the copy box and only confirmed the seal of the original box. That confirmation stood in the witness column of the three testaments.

**House.** The adopted-child line of the Ttukseom Heat-Source House (HC04). The steward was Park Nuri. Only half the key reached her.

**Relations.** Adopted child of Im Hajun. Stood between Yangjae Machineworks Company and the Sermon Registry.

## Yun Seorin

President of the Republic of Korea Government. The person who believed the seal was the state itself.

**Life.** The generation of 2126. Her hands came from the registration duty of the main building of the Government Seoul Complex at 209 Sejong-daero, Jongno-gu. The taboo of the Record Blade was forgery. Without her ratifying stamp, the water contract became paper. Yun Seorin became president after keeping the seal box of the Bukhansan Bogukmun Records House in an unbroken line. The seal box of the Seoul Station Record-Law House belonged to Yeorian. When the two boxes matched, one believed the state was one, but in the reconstruction era there were two boxes. The hand that claimed 「the Republic of Korea」 at Gwanghwamun Station borrowed water and rail. If she stopped Han Jaemok's western proposal, the debt piled up; if she stamped it, Yangcheon's veto died.

**Office.** Rank: President. Title: President of the Republic of Korea Government. Livelihood byname: Chief of the Record Bureau.

**Martial Path.** Record Blade.

**Anecdote.** When the three testaments were received, Yun Seorin suspended ratification and only confirmed the seal of the box. The suspension came one day before Han Jaemok's expansion proposal.

**House.** Bukhansan Bogukmun Records House (HC05). Steward. The steward of the Seoul Station Record-Law House (HP04) was Yeorian.

**Relations.** A ratification dispute with Han Jaemok. A ration-ledger exchange with Nam Yungyeong. A ratification rivalry with Jeong Yura.

## Park Taegyeom

The siding duty of the Yongsan Track Gate belonged to Jang Sehwa. When the two hands traded places with the seasons, the state looked like two. The Itaewon interpreters were an enclave within this state. That was why he opened the Post-State Council.

**Office.** Rank: Stationmaster. Title: Stationmaster of the Line Succession State. Livelihood byname: Railway Coordinator.

**Martial Path.** Wheel Hammer.

**Anecdote.** When Im Hajun's pump train stopped at the Yongsan warehouse, Park Taegyeom published the dispatch sheet and convened the Post-State Council. The convening came before the convoy.

**House.** Yongsan Convoy House (HC11). Steward. The steward of the Yongsan Track Gate (HP02) was Jang Sehwa.

**Relations.** Dispatch with Jang Sehwa. Im Hajun's pump train.

## O Haerin

**Life.** The generation of 2126. Her hands came from the Seoul temple and the Sotaesan Memorial Hall in Heukseok-dong, Dongjak-gu. She had no martial path. She had only a livelihood. The head temple in Iksan lay outside the city and could not enter a Seoul slot, and the temple clergy of Heukseok copied the canon by hand. O Haerin became Head of the Edification Office after keeping that hand-copy and the warehouse key of the Noryangjin Delivery House in an unbroken line. The cold-storage key of the Noryangjin Cold-Storage Guild belonged to Gu Haon. The market function was scrapped, but ice credit remained as a neighbor's debt. If Han Jaemok opened the prices, the warehouse showed; if he closed them, Yangcheon's taps ran dry first.

**Office.** Rank: Head of the Edification Office. Title: Head of the Edification Office of Won Buddhism. Livelihood byname: Head of the Cold-Storage Merchants.

**Martial Path.** None. Only a livelihood.

**Anecdote.** When the counterfeit medicine went up at auction, O Haerin raised the price with the box still sealed. The seal fell on the same day as Ryu Eunbi's refusal of the trade.

**House.** Noryangjin Delivery House (HC06). Steward. The steward of the Noryangjin Cold-Storage Guild (HP08) was Gu Haon.

**Relations.** A price-disclosure dispute with Han Jaemok. A market rivalry with Nam Yungyeong.

## Choi Jiwoo

## Baek On

## I Hongwon

President of the Seocho Computing Group. The person who saw the depot's seniority as the trial itself.

**Life.** The generation of 2126. The keys of cooling and computing at Samsung Town, Seocho-daero 74-gil, Seocho-gu. The trial wheel of the Sinjeong Wheel House belonged to Yu Sejin.
