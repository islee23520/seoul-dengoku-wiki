# Offices and Ranks

The titles of Seoul in 2126 were not game terms. They were seats people in Korea understood in 2026, or seats Joseon had used before that. For a hand to open water and stamp the registers after the call mesh died, it had to hold rank.

The king, duke, count, baron, and court figures of Crusader Kings remained as the five layers of land. The opening took those layers and changed only the forms of address to each nation's real 2026 institutional ranks. The tiers were identical across the sixteen states; only the names differed. No word that had called a baron a baron remained in the ledgers, and the hand charged with a single station was called by one of that nation's actual titles — watch chief, company commander, steward, or section head.

There was no emperor tier. Seoul had no son of heaven. The heads of the sixteen states did not call one another king. What they called each other was the tier-1 title of that nation; only the layer the ledgers assigned was of king rank.

## The Five Layers

The baron, count, duke, king, and court figures of Crusader Kings were layers of land. The opening fixed those layers as tiers 1 through 5 and wrote the names apart only by form of government and institutional rank. The feudal operating houses read from stationmaster down to jusa clerk; the military governments from commander down to byeongjang; the theocracies from Moderator, archbishop, supreme patriarch, and Kyujeongwon head down to gyosa, gyowoo, sindo, and gyodo; the commercial states from chairman, guild head, representative, or chairwoman down to jikwon, johapwon, hosongwon, and hoewon.

| Tier | CK mapping | Old Joseon office | Unit ruled |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | King | Suryeong | One of the sixteen states |
| 2 | Duke | Panseo | The great matters within a state — water, escort, registers, repair |
| 3 | Count | Gunsu | One gu, the living sphere of a great station |
| 4 | Baron | Hyeongam | One station, one sluice, one market |
| 5 | Court figures | Chamha | No land. Signature and watch only |

The cells the old ledgers had stamped as Suryeong, Panseo, Gunsu, Hyeongam, and Chamha kept only their tier numbers, with the title strings substituted from the national table. The basis of the rank distribution was the five layers of the current 984 cards — the Suryeong cell as tier 1, Panseo as tier 2, Gunsu as tier 3, Hyeongam as tier 4, Chamha as tier 5. No land was divided anew; only the names were fitted to institutional ranks.

## Offices by State

Real ranks were laid over the tiers. Feudal heredity, emergency superintendence, and laying-on-of-hands remained in the prose, while the titles themselves kept the words those institutions had used in 2026.

### Republic of Korea Government

President—Minister—Vice Minister—Director General—jusa clerk. The ranks the Government Complex Seoul main building had used. Tier 1 was called president, though elections were dead. The hands that had kept the Gwanghwamun Station seal chest and the conscription registers unbroken inherited that seat, while the Ministers, Vice Ministers, and Directors General divided the complex's great matters and the gu and stations among themselves. The jusa clerk held no land.

### Water Compact · Line Succession State

Stationmaster—Bureau Head—District Head—Watch Chief—jusa clerk. The watch grammar of the Yeongdeungpo Arisu Water Treatment Center under Seoul Arisu Headquarters and the Yongsan rail transshipment. The stationmaster's seat remained hereditary. The Yeongdeungpo Station sluice house and the Yongsan Station track house passed their keys to their grandsons, the Bureau Head approved the great matters of treatment and dispatch, the District Head fed the gu, and the Watch Chief opened one sluice, one storage track.

### Escort Protection State · Gate Military Government

Commander—Chief of Staff—Battalion Commander—Company Commander—byeongjang sergeant. The military ranks where the conscription remnants and the treatment watch were bound together. The Amsa Station commander read waterworks protection as emergency superintendence, and the Guui Station commander had still not gathered sluices and the Achasan outposts into one hand. The Chief of Staff bore the great matters within the state, the Battalion Commander the gu, the Company Commander one bridge or ridge, and the byeongjang the watch that carried a signature alone.

### Sermon Registry State

Moderator—Elder—Gwonsa—Deacon—gyosa teacher. The session ranks left by the Yeonji-dong General Assembly Hall; the opening capital was Samsung Station. The Moderator read the General Assembly registers through hereditary laying-on-of-hands, the Elder held the great relief within the state, the Gwonsa the living sphere, the Deacon one church, and the gyosa the landless signature.

### Cathedral Accreditation State

Archbishop—Priest—Brother—Deacon—gyowu fellow. The ranks of the Myeongdong Cathedral curia. The Bishops' Conference building on Myeonmok-ro, Gwangjin, remained only an outer warehouse.

### Sangha Relief State

## The Old Joseon Office Substitution

There had been a season when the opening draft called the five layers by the names of a Joseon county's chief seats. Suryeong, Panseo, Gunsu, Hyeongam, and Chamha were that draft's forms of address. The tier numbers and the units ruled stayed as they were, and only the titles moved to the real institutional ranks of the table above. The 2026 electoral words mayor, district head, and dong head were folded away with them. As long as water flowed, the living seat was tier 1, and its name was president, stationmaster, commander, Moderator, or chairman.

| Old address | Tier | CK mapping | Ground of substitution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Suryeong | 1 | King | The chief of a county. The head-of-state cell of one of the sixteen states. Substituted by each nation's tier-1 title |
| Panseo | 2 | Duke | The chief of a Six Boards office and the 2026 Bureau Head's approval. The cell of a state's great matters |
| Gunsu | 3 | Count | The seat that fed one gu. The living-sphere cell |
| Hyeongam | 4 | Baron | One station, one sluice. The stronghold cell |
| Chamha | 5 | Court figures | A seat with no land and a signature only. The working-rank cell |

The names of the Six Boards were Joseon's Personnel, Taxation, Rites, War, Punishments, and Works. What actually moved at the opening remained four matters: water and rations, escort and the muster registers, accreditation and punishment, repair and standards. The personnel database had died with the call mesh, and the rites went to the churches, the temples, the shrines, and the chapels. The 2026 HR and general-affairs teams were not laid onto those seats.

## Why These Offices

The Mayor of Seoul in 2026 had been the chief of one city, the district head the chief of one gu, the dong head the chief of one dong. After the revocation, when the databases went dark, what remained were the units people opened by hand — treatment plants, depots, stations, sluices, the complex, halls, churches. Those units resembled the counties of Joseon, so the draft borrowed the word Suryeong. The opening canon kept the layers but called a president at Gwanghwamun, a stationmaster at Yeongdeungpo, a commander at Amsa, a Moderator at Samsung Station, a chairman at Gangnam. The word mayor died with the 2026 election.

A signature skipping rank was void. When a tier 4 forged a tier 1's seal, accreditation opened. When a tier 5 played a tier 3, that station's water was cut. This was not a worldview ornament but the story's rules of approval. The approvals and refusals of [Ambitions](../characters/Ambitions-and-Relations.md) stood only upon these ranks.

## Title and Livelihood

A title was a layer of land. A livelihood was the work that person's hands did. A president could be a maintenance hand; a deacon could be a pastor — as in 2026 Korea a district head had been a deacon. Livelihoods were not raised into titles. The Water Director-General, the Fabrication Council Mediator, and the pump-technology steward were trade aliases; into the ledgers' cells went only the five titles of each nation's table.

## Succession

The three questions of Crusader Kings were used as they stood. Who is qualified. How is it divided. Who holds the guardianship. The answers differed by nation. Qualification was examination, heredity, or the pulpit's laying-on-of-hands. Division kept joint partition as the default; when a tier 1 died, water and escort could split. Guardianship opened only when a minor heir existed. The details belonged to each nation's charter and [the heir, name-roster, and world ledger](../characters/Heirs-Names-and-World-Ledger.md).
