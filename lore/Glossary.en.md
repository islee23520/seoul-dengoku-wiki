# 용어 사전 (Glossary)

This document is an encyclopedia that explains to readers the key concepts and terms frequently used in the 《서울:전국》 worldview. Korean headwords remain the primary keys; the English gloss follows each headword.

## States (the Sixteen)
- **급수계약정** — *Water Compact*: A city-state that held its power by monopolizing the purification and supply of water. (Alias: 급수정)
- **대한민국정부** — *Republic of Korea Government*: An old government organization that claimed to inherit the ruling system from before the Great Collapse. (Aliases: 정부, 옛정부)
- **정동노동총연맹** — *Jeongdong General Labor Union*: An armed autonomous league formed by the underground laborers of the Jeongdong area. (Alias: 정동연맹)

## Houses and Organizations
- **여의도전산가** — *Yeouido Computing House*: A corporate house that controlled the financial computing network and data centers of old Yeouido. (Alias: 전산가)
- **영등포수문가** — *Yeongdeungpo Sluice House*: A technician house that seized the underground waterways and control valves of the Yeongdeungpo area. (Alias: 수문가)

## Persons and Offices
- **통제권자** — *controller*: A senior post holder inside an organization who commanded tactical operations and resource distribution. (Alias: 통제관)
- **수문손** — *Sluice Hand*: An underground hydraulic worker who operated waterway valves or carried the related skills. The word served as common field slang. (Alias: 수리공)
- **렌즈숨** — *lens-breather*: One who lived always wearing lenses, synchronized with machines.

## Technology and Equipment
- **피지컬 에이아이 (PAI)** — *physical AI*: A machine intelligence with a physical body that carried out tactical operations.
- **렌즈** — *lens*: An augmentation device that overlaid battlefield information and machine status onto the wearer's field of view. (Alias: 전술렌즈)

## Martial Paths and Tactics
- **감응조준법** — *resonance aiming method*: A marksmanship technique that synchronized the sensor information of a physical AI lens with the shooter's own sight.
- **수문호흡법** — *sluice-breathing method*: A breathing technique that secured oxygen efficiently from the toxic-gas-laced air of the lower levels.

## Facilities and Geography
- **심층 터널** — *deep tunnel*: The deepest zone at the bottom of the transfer passages, where no light entered and no air moved.

## Events and Chronology
- **대붕괴** — *the Great Collapse*: The immense disaster that destroyed surface civilization and drove the survivors into the Seoul subway network. (Alias: 붕괴)
- **2126년** — *the year 2126*: The present point in time, after the Great Collapse, when the underground ecosystem stabilized and the sixteen states settled into a balance of power. (Alias: 현재)

## Diseases
- **포자감염** — *spore infection*: A fatal condition in which spores of underground fungi from cold environments lodged in the lungs and hardened the respiratory tract. (Alias: 포자병)
