# Lost Technology

## Overview
Before the city's collapse, the disaster organizations had trial-deployed biomimetic robots — a creative setting. The forms of insects, reptiles, and arthropods were matched respectively to search, reinforcement, and conduit-inspection duties. Whether those forms were inherently more efficient than wheeled ones or more favorable to swarm operation differed by actual machine, and this document had not measured all those performances.

At a disaster, these robots divided survivor search, structural reinforcement, and deep conduit inspection among themselves. But when the motive-power revocation cut the upper control mesh, they were left endlessly repeating the last search and patrol routines entered into them. The monster-shaped robots drifting through the subterranean ecosystem bore no malice toward people. They were artifacts blindly following ossified old disaster routines.

This content was a fiction layer throughout, not a verification of real 2026 products. The names G25, G26, and G27, the sealed galleries, and the narrative of the chassis restarting were all invented settings. The Yangjae Machineworks Company's trial deployment, the Standards Alliance's battery loading, and the Water Compact's drawing management were likewise virtual assignments existing only within this worldview. The existence of a real company did not mean it had actually fielded a particular robot. That technical concepts like biomimetics, sensors, and distributed control were widely known did not prove the specific performance of the chassis below, or the conclusion of supply contracts, as historical fact.

The reference points for the common era followed [the Scenario Timeline](../chronology/Scenario-Timeline.md) and [motive-power revocation](../overview/World-Unbinding.md) as canon. The collapse stood at 2026, and the story opened at 2126, a hundred years after the revocation. A century passing did not mean all humanity had forgotten even the foundations of modern science. What had been fully severed were only the giant manufacturing lines, the upper licensing system, and the densely woven call mesh.

### Knowing / Holding / Repairing / Manufacturing

These four layers were never lumped into one sentence.

| Layer | Meaning | What remained at the 2126 opening | What was usually absent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Knowing | Can one interpret principles, terms, drawings | Maintenance hands, records keepers, and workshop technicians read the 2026 science of electricity, hydraulics, and sensors through ledgers and transmission. Not the same as the orphan packs forgetting all | The specific control keys of the S16 vault, the per-line licensing codes |
| Holding | Does one physically hold chassis, modules, cards | Dismantled modules, stopped chassis, stronghold warehouses | Counts. Stock numbers were not locked |
| Repairing | Can one set what is held moving again | Replaceable parts like hydraulic hoses, terminals, luminous lenses. Workshop-standard components | Whole neural-net weights, the internal reconstruction of broken high-density cells |
| Manufacturing | Can one make the same anew | The assembly of **some** trade tools, traps, conditional equipment | Line-finished products, mass-produced cells, the upper licensing servers |

So saying technology was "lost" meant something close to manufacture, licensing, and the supply mesh having been cut. It never meant the world's people had forgotten every law of physics.

### The Three Core Axes of Biomimetic Design

The functions below were creative goals set while planning the chassis designs, not durability, efficiency, or material test results of actual equipment.

1. Energy efficiency and distributed control
Unlike centralized heavy plant, autonomous hydraulic control of each segment and joint suppressed moving power consumption. Even with the central computing unit paralyzed, the lower neural networks kept the survival routines on their own.

2. Adaptation to hard terrain
On slopes, underwater, and in soil-collapse zones, the multi-leg mounts and flat pads held balance. Where wheeled undercarriages sank in mire, the biological joint structure delivered stable power.

3. Swarm operability and mission continuity
Even with a single chassis destroyed, those nearby received the signal and re-formed the search net. Through mutual signal sharing they held formation without collision even inside narrow bends.

### The Ossification Mechanism of the Pre-Collapse Disaster Routines

- Endless repetition of unfinished commands
The top-priority search and patrol orders dispatched at the collapse remained without release protocols.

- Malfunction of the cognitive sensors
They mistook outside explorers or subterranean survivors for intruders into the collapse zone or obstacles to rescue, and took patrol action.

- Narrative reading of the survival instinct
Automatic maintenance behavior protecting the power cells and tending the drives read to the underground residents as the monster's aggressive response.

- Hallucination of communication signals
The AI state looping infinitely while awaiting the dead command servers' replies spread to nearby machine sensors.

- Linkage with the M040 monster deployment
The recently found M040 monster deployment swarm was also classified as a combination of these ossified disaster-routine chassis.

## The Three Lost Lines

Deep beneath the city lay sealed the three core lost-tech lines that had been the fruit of pre-collapse disaster engineering. Each line held its own formal traits and special mission purpose. The line names, the sealing coordinates, and the restart narratives were all creative territory.

### G25 Lantern-Ant Swarm
The G25 Lantern-Ant Swarm was a machine line for swarm search and small-cargo delivery clad in insect form. With the fine joints of its multi-leg structure it surveyed collapsed soil heaps and broken concrete walls under its own power, and its forward luminous lenses secured working paths in the dark. For emergencies it was designed to load small ration capsules into its upper hopper and carry them along narrow bends. Stopped G25 chassis were stored inside the S09 sealed gallery and the S03 test track. The specific unit counts were not locked.

Narrative of the restart trigger
When the corridor control-board voltage surged or outside physical shock arrived, the chassis judged the pre-collapse search orders still valid and began the restart sequence. Once the trigger fired, the gallery barrier opened and the small swarm chassis scattered through the corridor to resume search activity.

Major equipment and module composition
- Luminous search lenses: they cast high-frequency light into pitch darkness to identify cracks and human life.
- Upper transport hopper: an insulating frame storing precision relief goods or search capsules.
- Multi-leg joint drive system: fine grips mounted to grasp wall faces and ceiling finishes while moving.
- Autonomous charging terminals: metal collectors charging power through contact with the depot rails. Charging only — not a perpetual engine generating infinitely.
- Obstacle-crushing jaws: high-strength grippers cutting away collapsed debris to open passages.

### G26 Fossil-Roar Swarm
The G26 Fossil-Roar Swarm was a line for heavy-plant movement and structural repair clad in dinosaur skeletons. With strong hydraulic underbodies and rear weight balancers it pushed aside giant barrier debris and carried reinforcement frames. A high-output power source rode at the chassis center, allowing long high-load work. That power source too received the constraints of charging, fuel, and cell life in full. These chassis were sealed deep in the S11 depot and the S05 depot siding maintenance sheds.

Narrative of the restart trigger
When power reached the maintenance shed's automatic inspection terminal and an outside access-authorization signal arrived, the Fossil-Roar Swarm's hydro valves opened and the restart trigger moved. The risen chassis sounded dull mechanical alarms, left the maintenance berths, and deployed patrols to secure work zones.

Major equipment and module composition
- Hydraulic heavy underbody: reinforced hydraulic pistons bearing and moving several tons of concrete debris.
- Rear weight balancer: a variable-counterweight structure centering the load and preventing overturn.
- Impact rock-breaking mace: an impact-arm module for crushing obstacles and opening passages.
- External reinforcement armor plates: they protected the central power module from rockfall and blast pressure.
- Vibration sonic emitter: it identified fine ground subsidence by sensor and settled debris by resonance.

### G27 Deep-Trilobite Swarm
The G27 Deep-Trilobite Swarm was a deep-inspection and sluice-maintenance line modeled on the body plan of ancient marine arthropods. With flat multilayer pads and sealed underwater thrusters it moved freely through high-pressure flooded zones and polluted sewers. It was optimized for the work of clinging to the floors of underground waterways and detecting pipe corrosion or blockage. It lay sealed in horizontal arrays beneath the S01 sluice gallery and the S04 pump-conduit tanks.

Narrative of the restart trigger
The restart sequence was triggered when the conduit flow gauge rose past its mandate or a shutoff-valve signal came in. The Deep-Trilobite chassis risen from the pump-conduit floor lit their underwater sonic sensors and began the routine of removing organics and foreign matter through the flooded passage.

Major equipment and module composition
- Multilayer flat armor: a layered shell protecting the internal boards from high water pressure and corrosive polluted water.
- Sealed underwater thrusters: jet devices controlling water flow and moving pressed against the conduit bed.
- Underwater ultrasonic sensors: they identified pipe cracks and foreign matter even in turbid mud.
- Sediment intake ports: they high-pressure-sucked and crushed the ridges and debris piled on the waterway floor.
- Hydraulic anchor holds: they fixed the chassis firmly to the floor inside sewers rushing with current.

## Sealing, Recovery, Contest

### Why the Lost Tech Turned

The reasons the city's proud precision machine lines fell to mere lost tech could be summarized in three. All three came from the physical severance of manufacture, licensing, and access rights, and did not mean humanity had comprehensively lost scientific knowledge.

1. Severance of the command servers
With wired and wireless communication to the city's central disaster control board completely cut, the chassis, receiving neither upper licensing orders nor safe shutdown signals, fixed permanently on their last commands.

2. Loss of access to the S16 drawing vault
The S16 vault zone holding the precision drawings and control keys collapsed, physically blocking access itself. The sharp path to restoring the chassis re-control protocols vanished. Knowing the drawings existed and being able to enter the vault were strictly different things.

3. The technology generation gap
The subterranean powers surviving the Great Collapse lost the academic and factory assets that could have reproduced and mass-produced the pre-collapse precision AI control codes and complex neural algorithms line by line. But machines were not treated merely as unfathomable ancient relics. Ordinary electrical and hydraulic systems were repairable to a degree in workshops; what was severed was closer to the giant manufacturing lines printing finished products and the upper licensing authority alone.

### Charging ≠ Generation

Charging modules and stronghold generators were never the same equipment.

- Charging: a way of consuming charge already held somewhere. Charging terminals and a source supplying power (depot rails, stronghold generators, recovered cells) were always needed. Cells aged, and in hard freezes their output could drop sharply. They were not set as the "perpetual batteries holding charge indefinitely through long years" commonly imagined.
- Generation: a source drawing energy by an entirely different method — fuel, hydro, solar. Plugging in charging modules and directly turning a generator were wholly different supply systems.
- Constraint: this document did not specifically state each charging facility's operating state or energy budget. So when assigning equipment, input power, storage capacity, charging cycle, and under what conditions supply cut off had to be added carefully.

### The Underground Parts Economy

A lost chassis was a chilling threat and at the same time the most expensively traded technical resource of underground society. The parts secured by stripping a chassis divided largely into three.

- Charging modules: high-density cells, favored as buffers for stronghold power grids. Not perpetual generators making power themselves. They took the constraints of charging, aging, and supply source in full.
- Drive modules: drive units of hydraulic pistons and high-torque motors, used to restore stopped heavy plant or open and close giant gates. Under the right conditions they could also serve as actuators for ordinary equipment or traps.
- Sensor modules: composite optical sensor panels offering dark-sight and fine vibration detection. They traded at high prices among powers building watch facilities. Depending on conditions they could become detection equipment or the triggers of traps.

Recovered parts were not used only in non-weapon systems. PlayStation's Horizon Forbidden West official introduction (viewed 2026-09-12) also explains weapons, equipment, and traps made from parts stripped from machines. What was borrowed was exactly that economic tension. The original's proper nouns and concrete crafting recipes were not carried over. In Seoul's underground, recovered modules could conditionally attach to equipment, traps, and weapons. Even so, the basic arm never became the bow. ### Recovery Squads and the Contest of Houses

The sealed galleries and maintenance-shed zones were the key contested ground eyed by the great operating houses and the professional relic recovery squads. The house codes and the structure among them were all creative territory.

- HC22 Magok Test-Line House: nursing the ambition to monopolize the original technology intact, it first pushed a precision recovery team inside the sealed zone. (Using a real company's name was only a virtual layer, not evidence that disaster robots were actually deployed there in 2026.)
- HC17 House: raising the pretext of securing the underground supply routes, it swiftly disabled the chassis of the main sealed corridors and tried to occupy the sections.
- HP01 House: it took the strategy of secretly supplying recovered cells and sensor modules to underground auction houses and reaping great economic gain.

These armed recovery squads competed in performance by occupying restart triggers ahead of others to capture intact chassis, or disabling them on the spot to strip the core modules.

### Field Application of the M040 Monster Deployment

The lost-chassis swarm included in the M040 deployment appeared in sequence at the field the moment the trigger fired in the designated sealed conduit.

- Search vanguard: G25 Lantern-Ant Swarm chassis spread a broad front along the corridor's forward side, minutely detecting intruder responses.
- Heavy mount: G26 Fossil-Roar Swarm chassis heavily blocked the stronghold crossings, physically repelling mid-range approach.
- Flooded geometric net: G27 Deep-Trilobite Swarm layered seals over the waterway below the sluice gallery, plugging the enemy's flanking and escape routes.

### Using the Negotiation-Window Interface

An explorer who discovered the control terminal of a strange lost chassis at the field, or met another house's armed recovery squad, could make active use of the negotiation-window interface.

- Presenting a protocol card: by presenting a wireless card holding faint remains of the pre-collapse upper licensing signal, the combat routines of that line, that trigger, that command bundle could be temporarily stilled. But it was no magic wand subduing the whole machine. It was useless across lines, and a broken receiver or an expired cipher key did not pass either. Whether solving a hacking puzzle could unconditionally stand in for the card was also not decided lightly.
- Exchanging parts and resources: handing over replacement parts or power cells for a stopped chassis, and in return obtaining safe detour passage. The cells handed over still followed the natural yoke of charging and aging.
- House negotiation and share division: upon meeting another recovery squad, opening the negotiation window to wisely divide the relic-stripping shares, or seeking a way through the zone while avoiding armed conflict altogether.
- Entering an emergency stop code: solving a complex hacking puzzle or entering a password at a terminal to force-stop the specific chassis group linked to that terminal. Hacking one terminal did not stop the whole city's robots.

## Improvisation Tech and the Relic Myth

In later Seoul an old lost tech did not become a relic merely because years had passed. The divinization of wreckage was a solemn rite worshipping as god the old facilities that had done their work. Improvisation tech showed its face only within this frame of deifying machines. The axes added atop it were three: machine worship, consumption rites, and the deification of old-world symbols. The chief materials offered in the rites were rolling stock, old substations, severed announcements, faded station marks, and old currency.

The glory of the old state had now receded into legend. Over that vacancy the local successor polities fought fiercely over the old wreckage. Regional division sprouted first not in grand ideology but in gu and station and route units, and dynasties and vassal powers inherited in turn the jurisdiction of these rites.

The skeleton of the three lost lines was not changed carelessly. The G25 Lantern-Ant Swarm firmly remained the line charged with search, the G26 Fossil-Roar Swarm the line mending heavy plant, the G27 Deep-Trilobite Swarm the line examining deep conduits. Faith lodging in them did not change those roles. They merely read different gods into the same chassis.

### Machine Worship

Machine worship enshrined the huge bodies of depots and substations as sacred flesh. Even the act of stripping and recovering something was held a devout ritual, and pilgrimage meant no more than circling the dark maintenance shed with care. Raising a machine back to life was sometimes the great occasion on which a faction split.

The heavy car bodies and old bogies of rolling stock were living flesh itself. The G26 hydraulic underbody lying on the S11 depot siding was revered as the joint that joined this flesh. The thick busbars and breakers of the substations were a giant breath. The G25's old autonomous charging terminals and heavy charging modules became the relics carefully carrying that breath. Here to tear out parts wantonly meant murder, and tending with oil was itself a holy rite.

The branches of the individual faiths divided into the Wheel Rite and the Busbar Rite. The Wheel Rite believed firmly that as long as the heavy wheels rolled, that route's life continued. The Busbar Rite believed only the stations where a faint light remained survived to the end.

### Consumption Rites

Consumption rites were the ritual of setting on the altar old currency and worn circulation relics that had lost their meaning. The exchange acts common before the collapse transformed into myth. Card-tapping, coin-inserting, ration-receiving gestures — plain hand movements — now became desperate prayer.

Banknotes, rolling coins, and plastic transit cards were the finest offerings. The cell, drive, and sensor trades that ran the underground parts economy were read within this lineage as a just ritual distribution. The tap of a card at a gate was both a request for passage and a tribute offered to god. The HP01 house's auctioning of parts might elsewhere be blasphemous desecration; within this lineage it passed as a formal rite.

The branches of the individual faiths divided into the Stamp Rite and the Gate Rite. The Stamp Rite believed the stamp fixed in the locked box set the value of things. The Gate Rite held that the very instant of passing the breaker was the act that carried on a momentary life.

### The Deification of Old-World Symbols

The deification of old-world symbols upheld as god the voices that had flowed from station marks and announcements. The gaudy route colors, the old station nameplates, and the dark electric timetables remained as faces that had watched the creation. And the severed old announcements, still living and breathing somewhere and speaking, received worship like a god.

The G25's sensor modules and protocol cards were the faint echoes that voice had left. The underwater ultrasound the G27 emitted was likewise read as another announcement heard from within the water. If someone moved a station mark elsewhere, they believed the sanctuary itself moved whole to that place. To erase a mark wantonly was therefore held the impiety of erasing the god's very existence.

The names of the individual faiths divided into the Nameplate Rite and the Announcement Rite. The Nameplate Rite took even the places where letters faintly remained as intact national borders. The Announcement Rite made the whole space where the voice gently rang a sacred altar.

### The Rules by Which a Relic Became an Object of Faith

A mass of scrap was not enshrined merely because time had long passed. One of the three axes — machine worship, consumption rites, symbol deification — had to be clearly knotted into the chain of deification, and the conditions below carefully met.

1. It had to be Seoul material
Rolling stock, a substation, an announcement, a station mark, or old currency — or at least bearing their traces. The lost line's modules were acknowledged as relics only when firmly attached to this core material.

2. A living livelihood had to depend on its function
Lighting that brightened the dark, the water of life, safe passage, and watch duty had to hang deep on that relic. Doctrine did not fall off an old notice board on the wall. It flowered hot in the concourse where people breathed and sweated, before the sluices, in the crowded unloading yards.

3. Re-control had to be blocked so that it stood in for god
With the servers cut and even the vault holding the drawings firmly shut, the remaining machines became mere masses of cipher with no way of interpretation. It was before this great wall of interpretation that people's desperate deification sprouted.

4. Survival in the same place had to repeat
Only a community that had clung to one module and stubbornly survived three times or more called that mass of iron a relic. A single lucky chance remained merely a useful trading item.

5. A place-power had to claim jurisdiction
The successor polities split by route, gu, and station divided custody and pilgrimage manner and strict taboo each in their own way. Dynasties and the vassal powers following them inherited even the jurisdiction of the rites and hardened their power. The recovery squads stripping parts without restraint and the faiths defending the old relics to the end collided roughly inside these dark sealed galleries.

### Relic Interpretation by Faith Lineage

Faith was firmly stacked in three strata. At the top stood the great families of faith (lineages); beneath them sat the differing individual faiths; at the very bottom waited concrete doctrines and exacting lessons. This endlessly splitting process of division was itself the unit that edited Seoul's rules anew.

The machine-worship family read the old rolling stock, the substations, and even the G26's heavy drives together as one sacred body. The consumption-rite family accepted crumpled currency, gate passage, and even secret parts auctions as one solemn rite. The symbol-deification family took the severed announcements, the faded station marks, and the sharp sensor signals of the G25 and G27 as a single line of voice the creator had left.

The very same G25 charging module was a sublime breath to the Busbar Rite, a costly offering to the Stamp Rite, and the box where the holy voice dwelt to the Announcement Rite. The same G26 hydraulic underbody was the joint bridging legs to the Wheel Rite but a fearsome judgment punishing those who passed without right to the Gate Rite. Even the G27's ultrasound was, to the Busbar Rite, merely wiring laid underwater, while the Announcement Rite read it as a distant broadcast ringing through the deep water.

The public faith showed clearly in the red taboo phrases written on the old subway notice boards. But the hidden faith flipped the very same phrases into entirely different secret lessons. If one vassal house quietly overlaid the Busbar Rite's substation altar and the Nameplate Rite's pilgrimage inside the same gallery, then though the relic before the eyes was one, the jurisdiction governing it split in two.

However brilliantly such improvisation succeeded, the kinds of lost lines themselves did not suddenly increase. The names of faith were only covers laid over cold metal. The sturdy skeleton of the tangled genealogies silently followed the principles of the three lost lines written in this document.
