# Avatar asset library

This directory is the canonical binary library consumed and validated by `TOOL/avatar-gen`.

## Contents

- `components/`: shared eye mesh and accepted male/female oral assemblies
- `deliverables/`: six male/female body packages in editable Blender and external-texture FBX form
- `textures/`: canonical male and female 4K source-color textures
- `showcase/`: six-model comparison scene and selected views
- `evidence/renders/`: selected multi-light renders
- `evidence/acceptance/`: machine-readable approval receipts
- `manifest.json`: portable identity and ownership contract

The anatomical variants are provisional art bases, not medical references. Toon materials are project-authored and do not claim official commercial-game shader code. Rigging and animation are not included.
